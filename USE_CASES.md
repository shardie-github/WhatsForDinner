# Use Cases: Real Problems, Real Solutions

## 1. The 6 PM Fridge Stare

**The Scenario**: It's 6 PM. You're tired. You open the fridge and see... stuff. Eggs, some vegetables, leftover chicken, random condiments. You know you can make something, but what? You don't have time to search through recipes. You're hungry now.

**How What's for Dinner Solves It**: 
- Open the app
- Scan or manually add what's in your fridge
- Get 3 instant meal suggestions using exactly what you have
- Pick one, follow the recipe, start cooking

**The Outcome**: 
- Dinner decision made in under 2 minutes
- No wasted food
- No takeout order
- A satisfying meal you actually made

**Value**: Saves time, reduces waste, eliminates decision paralysis

## 2. The Weekly Meal Planning Marathon

**The Scenario**: Every Sunday, you spend 2 hours planning meals for the week. You search recipes, write shopping lists, try to balance nutrition and preferences. By Wednesday, the plan falls apart because you forgot an ingredient or changed your mind.

**How What's for Dinner Solves It**:
- Tell the app your dietary preferences and cooking time availability
- Get a complete week's meal plan generated in 30 seconds
- Shopping list automatically created with all ingredients
- Meal plans adapt if you skip a meal or change your mind

**The Outcome**:
- Meal planning takes minutes, not hours
- Shopping lists are complete and accurate
- Meal plans are flexible and adaptable
- You actually stick to the plan

**Value**: Saves 2+ hours per week, reduces shopping trips, increases meal plan adherence

---

## 3. The Expiring Food Crisis

**The Scenario**: You open your fridge and find tomatoes that expired yesterday, milk that expires tomorrow, and vegetables you forgot you bought. You feel guilty about the waste but don't know what to make with them.

**How What's for Dinner Solves It**:
- App tracks expiration dates (manual entry or receipt import)
- Get alerts 2 days before items expire
- Receive recipe suggestions specifically for expiring ingredients
- See "use it up" meal suggestions based on what's expiring soon

**The Outcome**:
- Use food before it expires
- Reduce food waste significantly
- Discover new recipes using ingredients you already have
- Feel good about reducing waste

**Value**: Saves $1,500+ per year in wasted food, reduces environmental impact, eliminates guilt

---

## 4. The Dietary Restriction Challenge

**The Scenario**: You're vegetarian, gluten-free, and trying to eat more protein. Every recipe app shows you options that don't fit your restrictions. You spend forever filtering and still end up with recipes that require ingredients you can't eat.

**How What's for Dinner Solves It**:
- Set your dietary restrictions once (vegetarian, gluten-free, high protein)
- All meal suggestions automatically filter to match your needs
- Pantry tracking ensures suggestions only use ingredients you can eat
- Nutrition information shows macros for every meal

**The Outcome**:
- Every suggestion fits your dietary needs
- No more filtering or searching
- Easy to track nutrition goals
- Discover new recipes that actually work for you

**Value**: Saves time searching, increases dietary compliance, makes meal planning enjoyable

## 5. The Family Meal Coordination

- Generate meal plans that work for the whole family
- Kid-friendly filter for picky eaters

- Meal plans sync across all family members' devices

**The Outcome**:
- Meals that everyone will eat

- Family members can contribute ideas

## 6. The Budget-Conscious Shopper

- Shopping lists show estimated costs
- "Budget-friendly" filter for affordable recipes
- Track what you actually use vs. what you buy

**The Outcome**:
- Stick to your grocery budget
- Buy ingredients you actually use
- Discover affordable meal options
- Better value from grocery spending

**Value**: Saves money on groceries, reduces unused purchases, maximizes ingredient value
