## 📱 Push Notifications

### Morning (7-9 AM)

**Proactive Planning:**
- "Want help planning tonight's dinner? We've got 3 suggestions ready."
- "Good morning! Your dinner plan for tonight is ready. Want to customize it?"
- "Quick reminder: Plan your week in 2 minutes and relax all weekend."

**Habit Reinforcement:**
- "Sunday planning mode: Prep once, relax all week."
- "Your weekly meal plan is ready. Tap to see your personalized suggestions."

### Mid-Day (12-2 PM)

**Value-Driven:**
- "Lunch break? Plan tonight's dinner in 30 seconds."
- "New recipes in your 'Quick & Easy' category. Want to see?"

### Evening (4-6 PM)

**Dinner Reminders:**
- "Tonight's dinner suggestion: [Recipe Name]. Tap to see full plan."
- "Don't forget: You planned [Recipe] for tonight. Need the grocery list?"
- "Dinner's ready. (Well, the plan is.) Tap to see your recipes."

### Weekend (Saturday-Sunday)

**Weekly Planning:**
- "Weekend planning mode: Plan your week in 2 minutes."
- "Your weekly meal plan is ready. Want to customize?"
- "Prep once, relax all week. Plan your meals now."

### Gamification (Surprise)

**Achievements:**
- "🎉 You've planned 10 meals! Unlock 20% off premium recipes."
- "🔥 7-day planning streak! You're on fire!"
- "New badge unlocked: Weekend Warrior! Plan 5 weekend meals to earn it."

**Seasonal/Events:**
- "Summer Grill Mode unlocked! 🔥 Check out 10 new grill recipes."
- "Holiday Cook-Off starts Monday! Join 500+ planners for Thanksgiving prep."

### Re-engagement (Dormant Users)

**7 Days Inactive:**
- "We miss you! Here's a quick meal plan for this week. No pressure, just here to help."
- "Your meal planning assistant is still here. Want to plan this week's dinners?"

**30 Days Inactive:**
- "What's for Dinner has some new features. Want to see what's changed?"
- "Your meal planning assistant is still here when you need it. No judgment, just help."

### Notification Tone Guidelines

**✅ Do:**
- Warm, helpful, supportive
- "Here to help" language
- Clear value proposition
- Specific, actionable

**❌ Don't:**
- Pushy, guilt-inducing
- Generic, vague
- "You forgot again" tone
- Excessive urgency (unless time-bound offer)
