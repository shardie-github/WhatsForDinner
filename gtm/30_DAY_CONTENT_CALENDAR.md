## 📝 Content Checklist (Daily)

**Before Posting:**
- [ ] Copy aligns with brand voice
- [ ] Visual is clear and on-brand
- [ ] CTA is clear and actionable
- [ ] Hashtags are relevant (5-10)
- [ ] Link in bio is updated (if applicable)
- [ ] Post timing is optimized (peak hours)

**After Posting:**
- [ ] Monitor engagement (first hour)
- [ ] Reply to comments (within 2 hours)
- [ ] Track performance (24 hours)
- [ ] Adjust strategy based on performance
