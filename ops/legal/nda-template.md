---

### 1. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

"Confidential Information" means all non-public, proprietary information disclosed by either party, including but not limited to:

- Business plans, financial information, revenue data
- Product specifications, technical documentation
- Customer lists, marketing strategies
- Trade secrets, proprietary processes
- Any information marked "Confidential" or "Proprietary"

**Exclusions:**
- Information already publicly known
- Information independently developed
- Information lawfully received from third party
- Information required by law to disclose

---

### 2. OBLIGATIONS

Each party agrees to:

1. **Keep Confidential:** Not disclose Confidential Information to third parties
2. **Use Only for Purpose:** Use Confidential Information only for stated purpose
3. **Protect:** Take reasonable steps to protect Confidential Information
4. **Return/Destroy:** Return or destroy Confidential Information upon request

---

### 3. TERM

This agreement is effective from [DATE] and continues for [2 YEARS] unless terminated earlier.

**Survival:** Confidentiality obligations survive termination for [2 YEARS].

---

### 4. REMEDIES

Breach of this agreement may result in:
- Injunctive relief (court order to stop breach)
- Monetary damages
- Legal costs

## NOTES FOR USE

1. **One-Way vs. Mutual:** This is a mutual NDA (both parties share confidential info). For one-way NDAs (only you sharing), modify Section 2 to apply only to receiving party.

2. **When to Use:**
   - Investor meetings
   - Vendor evaluations
   - Partnership discussions
   - Contractor work (if needed)

3. **When NOT to Use:**
   - Public information
   - Already-public discussions
   - Standard vendor relationships (usually not needed)

4. **Storage:** Keep signed NDAs securely (encrypted digital or physical).
