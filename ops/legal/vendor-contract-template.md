### 3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**Ownership:**
- Upon full payment, Client owns all work product and deliverables
- Vendor retains right to use work in portfolio (unless otherwise agreed)

**License:**
- Vendor grants Client exclusive, perpetual license to use deliverables
- Client may modify deliverables as needed

---

### 4. CONFIDENTIALITY

Vendor agrees to:
- Keep all Client information confidential
- Not disclose Client information to third parties
- Return or destroy confidential materials upon request

---

### 5. WARRANTIES

Vendor warrants that:
- Work will be performed professionally
- Work will not infringe third-party rights
- Work will be original (unless otherwise specified)

---

### 6. TERMINATION

Either party may terminate this agreement:
- With 30 days written notice
- Immediately for breach of contract

**Upon Termination:**
- Vendor will deliver completed work
- Client will pay for work completed to date

---

### 7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

Vendor's liability is limited to the total amount paid under this agreement.
