# Seed Prep Playbook — Canadian Venture Operations

**Goal:** Prepare for seed fundraising round ($250K-$1M CAD) with all necessary materials and processes

---

## 🎯 Overview

This playbook covers everything needed to raise a seed round in Canada, including pitch deck, investor CRM, due diligence materials, and outreach strategy.

**Timeline:** 3-6 months from start to close  
**Target:** $250K-$1M CAD seed round

---

## 📋 Pre-Fundraising Checklist

### 1. Product Readiness
- [ ] MVP launched and generating revenue
- [ ] 50+ paying customers (or clear path to 100)
- [ ] Product-market fit indicators positive
- [ ] Key metrics trending upward

### 2. Financial Readiness
- [ ] Financial model built (3-year projection)
- [ ] Unit economics calculated (CAC, LTV, payback period)
- [ ] Burn rate understood ($X/month)
- [ ] Runway calculated (months until out of cash)

### 3. Legal Readiness
- [ ] Company incorporated (Canadian corporation)
- [ ] Cap table clean (founders, advisors, early employees)
- [ ] IP ownership clear (all code/docs owned by company)
- [ ] Terms of service, privacy policy in place

### 4. Team Readiness
- [ ] Founding team complete (co-founder, technical lead)
- [ ] Advisor network established (2-3 advisors)
- [ ] Board structure defined (if applicable)

## 🚀 Investor Pitch Deck Template

**Slide Structure (12-15 slides):**

### Slide 1: Title
- Company name: "What's for Dinner?"
- Tagline: "AI-powered meal planning for Canadians"
- Founder name(s)
- Contact info

### Slide 2: Problem
- "Canadians spend 5+ hours per week meal planning"
- "Generic meal apps don't understand Canadian grocery stores"
- "No solution for busy families with dietary restrictions"

### Slide 3: Solution
- "AI-powered meal planner that learns your preferences"
- "Integrates with Canadian grocery stores (Loblaws, Metro, Sobeys)"
- "30-second meal plan generation"

### Slide 4: Market Opportunity
- TAM: $X billion (total addressable market)
- SAM: $X million (serviceable addressable market)
- SOM: $X million (serviceable obtainable market)
- Focus: Canadian households (15M+)

### Slide 5: Product Demo
- Screenshots of key features
- User flow diagram
- Value proposition highlighted

### Slide 6: Traction
- Revenue: $X/month MRR
- Users: X active users
- Growth: X% month-over-month
- Key metrics: CAC, LTV, Churn

### Slide 7: Business Model
- Freemium: Free + Premium ($9.99/month)
- Revenue streams: Subscriptions, partnerships
- Unit economics: CAC $X, LTV $X, payback period X months

### Slide 8: Go-to-Market Strategy
- Customer acquisition: Content marketing, social media, partnerships
- Channels: App stores, website, partnerships
- Growth tactics: Referral program, influencer partnerships

### Slide 9: Competitive Landscape
- Competitors: Meal prep apps, recipe apps
- Differentiation: AI-powered, Canadian-focused, pantry-first
- Competitive advantages: Technology, local partnerships, speed

### Slide 10: Team
- Founder(s): Background, relevant experience
- Advisors: Names, expertise
- Hires planned: Roles, timeline

### Slide 11: Financial Projections
- 3-year revenue projection
- Key assumptions
- Path to profitability

### Slide 12: Ask
- **Amount:** $500K CAD seed round
- **Use of Funds:**
  - 40% Product development
  - 30% Marketing & growth
  - 20% Team hiring
  - 10% Operations
- **Milestones:** What you'll achieve with this funding

### Slide 13: Why Now
- Market timing (post-COVID meal planning trends)
- Technology readiness (AI maturity)
- Canadian market opportunity

## 📊 Investor CRM Setup

### Google Sheets: "Investor Pipeline"

**Columns:**
- Name
- Company/Fund
- Email
- LinkedIn
- Stage (Cold, Warm, Interested, Meeting Scheduled, Due Diligence, Closed, Passed)
- First Contact Date
- Last Contact Date
- Notes
- Next Steps
- Status

**Sample Data:**
```
Name,Company/Fund,Email,LinkedIn,Stage,First Contact Date,Last Contact Date,Notes,Next Steps,Status
John Smith,ABC Ventures,john@abc.com,linkedin.com/in/john,Interested,2025-01-10,2025-01-15,Met at event,Follow up next week,Active
```

### Views:
- **All Investors:** Default view
- **Hot Leads:** Stage = "Interested" OR "Meeting Scheduled"
- **Due Diligence:** Stage = "Due Diligence"
- **Follow-ups This Week:** Last Contact Date < 7 days ago

## 📧 Investor Outreach Email Templates

### Template 1: Cold Outreach

**Subject:** Seed Round: AI-Powered Meal Planning for Canadians

**Body:**
```
Hi [Investor Name],

I'm [Your Name], founder of "What's for Dinner?" - an AI-powered meal planning app for Canadians.

We're raising a $500K CAD seed round and I'd love to share our pitch deck.

**Traction:**
- $X/month MRR (growing X% MoM)
- X active users
- Product-market fit indicators positive

**Ask:** 15-minute call to discuss opportunity

[Pitch Deck Link]

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### Template 2: Warm Introduction Request

**Subject:** Introduction Request: Seed Round

**Body:**
```
Hi [Mutual Connection],

Hope you're well! I'm raising a $500K CAD seed round for "What's for Dinner?" and would love an introduction to [Investor Name] at [Fund Name].

[Brief 2-sentence pitch]

Would you be open to making an intro?

Thanks,
[Your Name]
```

### Template 3: Follow-up

**Subject:** Following up: Seed Round Opportunity

**Body:**
```
Hi [Investor Name],

Following up on my email from [Date]. We're raising a $500K CAD seed round and I'd love to share our progress:

[Update: e.g., "We've grown to $X/month MRR"]

Would you be open to a quick call?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## 📁 Due Diligence Data Room

**Create Folder:** "Investor Data Room"

**Contents:**
1. **Pitch Deck** (PDF)
2. **Financial Model** (Excel)
3. **Cap Table** (Excel)
4. **Term Sheet** (if applicable)
5. **Legal Documents:**
   - Articles of Incorporation
   - Shareholder Agreement
   - IP Assignment Agreements
   - Terms of Service
   - Privacy Policy
6. **Product Materials:**
   - Demo video
   - Screenshots
   - User testimonials
7. **Team:**
   - Founder bios
   - Advisor bios
   - Team structure
8. **Market Research:**
   - Competitive analysis
   - Market size research
   - User research

**Access:** Share via password-protected Google Drive or Dropbox
