## 📋 Email Best Practices

1. **Personalize:** Always customize [BRACKETED] sections
2. **Keep It Short:** 3-5 paragraphs max
3. **Clear Ask:** State what you want (meeting, intro, investment)
4. **Include Traction:** Always mention metrics
5. **Follow Up:** Send follow-up if no response after 1 week
