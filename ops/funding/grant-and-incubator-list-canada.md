# Grant and Incubator List — Canada 2025

**Goal:** Comprehensive list of active Canadian grants, incubators, and accelerator programs for tech startups

---

## 🎯 Overview

This document lists active Canadian funding programs, grants, and incubators available in 2025. Focus on programs supporting tech startups, particularly SaaS and consumer apps.

**Last Updated:** January 2025  
**Currency:** All amounts in CAD unless noted

---

## 💰 Government Grants

### 1. IRAP (Industrial Research Assistance Program)

**Program:** IRAP - Innovation Assistance Program  
**Eligibility:** Small-medium enterprises (SMEs) with < 500 employees  
**Funding:** Up to $50K-$500K  
**Timeline:** 6-12 months  
**Focus:** Technology development, commercialization  
**Website:** https://nrc.canada.ca/en/support-technology/industrial-research-assistance-program-irap

**Application Process:**
- Contact IRAP advisor
- Submit proposal
- Review and approval (3-6 months)

**Best For:** Early-stage tech startups with R&D component

---

### 2. SR&ED (Scientific Research and Experimental Development)

**Program:** SR&ED Tax Credit  
**Eligibility:** Canadian companies conducting R&D  
**Funding:** Up to 35% of eligible R&D expenses (refundable tax credit)  
**Timeline:** Annual tax filing  
**Focus:** R&D activities  
**Website:** https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/scientific-research-experimental-development-tax-incentive-program.html

**Application Process:**
- File annual tax return
- Claim SR&ED expenses
- CRA review and approval

**Best For:** Companies with significant R&D spending

---

### 3. Canada Digital Adoption Program (CDAP)

**Program:** CDAP - Boost Your Business Technology  
**Eligibility:** Small-medium businesses  
**Funding:** Up to $15K grant + $100K loan  
**Timeline:** 6-12 months  
**Focus:** Digital transformation  
**Website:** https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-digital-adoption-program/en

**Application Process:**
- Online application
- Assessment
- Approval and funding

**Best For:** Businesses adopting new technology

---

### 4. Futurpreneur Canada

**Program:** Startup Loan Program  
**Eligibility:** Entrepreneurs 18-39 years old  
**Funding:** Up to $60K loan (no interest for first year)  
**Timeline:** 3-6 months  
**Focus:** Young entrepreneurs  
**Website:** https://www.futurpreneur.ca/

**Application Process:**
- Online application
- Business plan submission
- Mentor matching
- Loan approval

**Best For:** Young founders (18-39)

---

### 5. BDC Seed Financing

**Program:** BDC Seed Financing  
**Eligibility:** Early-stage tech startups  
**Funding:** $100K-$5M  
**Timeline:** 3-6 months  
**Focus:** Tech startups  
**Website:** https://www.bdc.ca/en/financing/seed-financing

**Application Process:**
- Online application
- Pitch presentation
- Due diligence
- Funding approval

**Best For:** Tech startups ready for seed funding

**Eligibility:** Any startup  
**Funding:** $500K USD ($125K for 7% equity + $375K uncapped SAFE)  
**Timeline:** 3-month program  
**Focus:** Tech startups  
**Website:** https://www.ycombinator.com/

- Online application
- Video interview
- Batch acceptance

**Best For:** Ambitious tech startups (accepts Canadian companies)

## 🎯 Application Strategy

### Phase 1: Non-Dilutive (Grants)
1. Apply for IRAP (if R&D-focused)
2. Claim SR&ED tax credits (annual)
3. Apply for CDAP (if digital adoption)

### Phase 2: Dilutive (Equity)
1. Apply to accelerators (Y Combinator, Techstars)
2. Pitch to BDC Seed Financing
3. Join incubator program (DMZ, CDL)

### Phase 3: Debt Financing
1. Futurpreneur loan (if eligible)
2. BDC loans (if needed)
