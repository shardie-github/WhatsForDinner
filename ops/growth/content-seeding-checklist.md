- Social media posts (Instagram, Twitter/X, Facebook, LinkedIn)

## 🚀 Content Creation Workflow

### Step 1: Topic Research

**Tools:**
- Google Trends (search "meal planning canada")
- AnswerThePublic (question research)
- Reddit (r/mealprep, r/EatCheapAndHealthy)
- Competitor content analysis

**Topics to Cover:**
- "How to meal plan for a family of 4"
- "Best meal prep containers Canada"
- "Canadian grocery store price comparison"
- "Meal planning on a budget"
- "Keto meal prep ideas"
- "Vegan meal planning guide"

---

### Step 2: Content Creation

**Blog Posts (SEO-Optimized):**

**Template:**
```
Title: [Keyword] - [Benefit] (e.g., "Meal Planning for Busy Parents - Save 5 Hours Per Week")

Introduction (100 words)
- Hook: Problem statement
- Solution: Brief overview
- Promise: What reader will learn

Body (800-1200 words)
- H2: Main point 1
  - Bullet points
  - Examples
- H2: Main point 2
  - Bullet points
  - Examples
- H2: Main point 3
  - Bullet points
  - Examples

Conclusion (100 words)
- Recap key points
- CTA: "Try What's for Dinner? Free"

SEO:
- Target keyword: "meal planning canada"
- Meta description: 155 characters
- Alt text for images
- Internal links to app pages
```

**Social Media Posts:**

**Instagram Template:**
```
[Eye-catching image]

[Engaging caption]
- Hook (first line)
- Value proposition
- CTA: "Try free → link in bio"

Hashtags:
#mealprepcanada #torontofoodie #canadianfoodblogger #mealplanning #mealprepideas #healthyeating #mealprepsunday #mealprepideas #mealprep #mealplanning #whatsfordinner #torontofood #vancouverfood
```

---

### Step 3: Content Distribution

**Automated Publishing:**

**Zapier Integration:**

1. **Trigger:** Google Sheets → New Row (Content Calendar)
2. **Action:** Buffer/Hootsuite → Schedule Post
   - Platform: Instagram/Twitter/Facebook
   - Content: From spreadsheet
   - Scheduled Time: From spreadsheet

**Manual (If No Automation):**
- Use Buffer free tier (10 posts/platform)
- Or Hootsuite free tier
- Schedule weekly content batch

---

### Step 4: Content Repurposing

**One Piece of Content → Multiple Formats:**

**Example: Blog Post "5 Meal Planning Mistakes"**

1. **Blog Post:** Full 1200-word article
2. **Instagram Carousel:** 5 slides, one mistake per slide
3. **Twitter Thread:** 5 tweets, one mistake per tweet
4. **YouTube Short:** 60-second video summarizing mistakes
5. **Email Newsletter:** Excerpt + link to full post
6. **Pinterest Pin:** Infographic version

## 📊 Content Performance Tracking

**Metrics to Track:**

| Metric | Target | Dashboard |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Blog Traffic | > 1000/month | Google Analytics |
| Social Engagement | > 3% | Instagram Insights |
| Email Open Rate | > 25% | Email platform |
| Conversion Rate | > 2% | Google Analytics |
| SEO Rankings | Top 10 | Google Search Console |

**Dashboard:** Google Sheets "Content Performance"

**Columns:**
- Content Title
- Type (Blog, Social, Video)
- Published Date
- Views/Impressions
- Engagement (Likes, Comments, Shares)
- Click-Through Rate
- Conversions (Signups)
