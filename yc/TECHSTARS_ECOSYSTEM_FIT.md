## Techstars Application Positioning

### One-Liner
"AI-powered meal planning app that learns your pantry and generates personalized recipes in 30 seconds, eliminating daily decision fatigue for 50M Americans."

### Problem Statement
"Every day at 6 PM, 50 million Americans face the same question: 'What should I cook tonight?' Current solutions fail because they don't start with what users actually have in their pantry."

### Solution
"What's for Dinner combines AI-powered meal suggestions with smart pantry tracking to give you personalized recommendations based on what you have, your dietary preferences, and how much time you have to cook."

### Why Now
- **AI Maturity**: GPT-4 enables high-quality recipe generation
- **Consumer Behavior**: Post-COVID, more people cooking at home
- **Food Waste Awareness**: 40% of food wasted in US households
- **Mobile-First**: Smartphone penetration enables pantry scanning

### Traction
- ✅ Core product live (recipe generation working)
- ✅ Universal platform (web + mobile)
- ✅ Enterprise infrastructure (multi-tenant, compliance-ready)
- 🔄 Next 90 days: 10K users, $10K MRR, 40% weekly retention
