## The Problem (30 seconds)

**Every day at 6 PM, 50 million Americans face the same question: "What should I cook tonight?"**

This creates:
- **Decision fatigue**: 15+ minutes wasted deciding what to make
- **Food waste**: 40% of food wasted in US households ($1,500/year average)
- **Takeout dependency**: Defaulting to expensive delivery when overwhelmed
- **Repetitive meals**: Same recipes week after week

**Current solutions fail** because they don't start with what users actually have in their pantry.

---

## Our Solution (60 seconds)

**What's for Dinner** is an AI-powered meal planning app that:
1. **Learns your pantry** (barcode scanning, manual entry, receipt import)
2. **Generates personalized recipes** in 30 seconds based on what you have
3. **Adapts to your preferences** (dietary restrictions, cooking skill, family tastes)
4. **Gets smarter with use** (learns pantry patterns, cooking style, preferences)

**Key Differentiator**: Pantry-first approach (start with what you have, not what you need)
