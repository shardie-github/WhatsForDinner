# Competing Alternatives: What's for Dinner

**Purpose**: List alternatives users consider and why we win  
**Last Updated**: 2025-01-27

---

## Alternative 1: Takeout Apps (UberEats, DoorDash)

### Job They Solve
**Job**: "Get food fast"  
**How**: Order food delivery  
**Users**: Busy people who don't want to cook

### Why We Win
- **Cheaper**: $9.99/month vs $30-50 per order
- **Healthier**: Home-cooked meals vs restaurant food
- **Uses Existing Ingredients**: Reduces waste vs buying more food
- **Time-Saving**: 30 seconds to recipe vs 30 minutes for delivery

### Our Advantage
**Pantry-First**: Start with what users have, not what they need to buy

---

## Alternative 2: Recipe Sites (AllRecipes, Food Network)

### Job They Solve
**Job**: "Find recipes"  
**How**: Search recipes by keyword  
**Users**: People who know what they want to cook

### Why We Win
- **Starts with What You Have**: Pantry-first vs recipe-first
- **Personalization**: AI learns preferences vs generic recipes
- **Time-Saving**: 30 seconds to recipe vs 15-20 min searching
- **Reduces Waste**: Uses existing ingredients vs buying new ones

### Our Advantage
**Pantry-First**: Eliminates decision fatigue, reduces waste

---

## Alternative 3: Meal Apps (Yummly, Mealime)

### Job They Solve
**Job**: "Plan meals"  
**How**: Weekly meal planning, shopping lists  
**Users**: People who plan ahead

### Why We Win
- **Solves "Tonight" Problem**: What's for dinner TONIGHT vs plan ahead
- **Pantry-First**: Starts with what you have vs what you need
- **Faster**: 30 seconds to recipe vs 30 min planning
- **Less Planning Fatigue**: No planning required vs planning ahead

### Our Advantage
**Tonight-Focused**: Solves "what's for dinner TONIGHT?" not "plan ahead"

---

## Alternative 4: Generic AI (ChatGPT)

### Job They Solve
**Job**: "Get recipe ideas"  
**How**: AI chatbot suggests recipes  
**Users**: People who want AI suggestions

### Why We Win
- **Learns Preferences**: AI personalization vs generic suggestions
- **Pantry Integration**: Uses existing ingredients vs generic recipes
- **Better UX**: App vs chatbot
- **Family Features**: Family sharing vs individual use

### Our Advantage
**Personalization**: AI learns preferences, creates switching cost

---

## Competitive Positioning

### Our Unique Position
**Pantry-First + AI Personalization + Universal Platform**

### Why This Works
- **Pantry-First**: Unique in market (competitors are recipe-first)
- **AI Personalization**: Creates switching cost (competitors don't learn)
- **Universal Platform**: Better UX (competitors are single-platform)
