# PLG Growth Loops: What's for Dinner

**Purpose**: Document viral loops (shares → signups → shares)  
**Last Updated**: 2025-01-27

---

## Growth Loop 1: Recipe Sharing

### Loop Flow
1. User generates recipe
2. User shares recipe (social media, email)
3. Friend sees recipe, signs up
4. Friend generates recipe, shares it
5. Loop continues (exponential growth)

### Metrics
- **Share Rate**: 10%+ (users who share recipes)
- **Conversion Rate**: 5%+ (signups / shares)
- **Viral Coefficient**: 0.1+ (signups per user)

---

## Growth Loop 2: Family Sharing

### Loop Flow
1. User invites family member
2. Family member signs up, uses app
3. Family member invites friends
4. Friends sign up, use app
5. Loop continues (network effects)

### Metrics
- **Family Sharing Adoption**: 30%+ (users who invite family)
- **Conversion Rate**: 30%+ (family signups / invites)
- **Viral Coefficient**: 0.2+ (signups per user)

## Growth Loop 3: Recipe Quality → Social Proof

### Loop Flow
1. User generates recipe
2. Recipe is good (high rating)
3. User shares recipe (social proof)
4. Friends see social proof, sign up
5. Loop continues (social proof → signups)

### Metrics
- **Recipe Quality**: 4+ star average rating
- **Share Rate**: 10%+ (users who share recipes)
- **Conversion Rate**: 5%+ (signups / shares)
