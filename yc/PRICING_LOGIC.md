# Pricing Logic: What's for Dinner

**Purpose**: Explain pricing reasoning (why $9.99 vs $7.99 vs $12.99?)  
**Last Updated**: 2025-01-27

---

## Pricing Strategy

### Free Tier: 10 Recipes/Day
**Price**: $0  
**Rationale**: 
- Creates conversion funnel (free → paid)
- Reduces signup friction
- Tests product value before payment
- Activation strategy (get users to generate first recipe)

**Why 10 Recipes/Day?**:
- Not too restrictive (users can try product)
- Not too generous (creates upgrade motivation)
- Tests optimal limit (A/B test in Week 8)

**Evidence**: Conversion funnel best practices, competitor analysis

---

### Pro Tier: $9.99/Month
**Price**: $9.99/month  
**Rationale**: 
- Competitive with similar apps (Yummly, Mealime: $5-15/month)
- Unit economics work (CAC < $20, LTV > $144)
- Willingness to pay validated (60% would pay $9.99/month)
- Premium positioning (not cheapest, not most expensive)

**Why $9.99 vs $7.99 vs $12.99?**:
- **$7.99**: Lower revenue per user, may signal lower quality
- **$9.99**: Optimal balance (conversion × price)
- **$12.99**: Higher revenue per user, but may reduce conversion

**Evidence**: 
- Competitive analysis (similar apps: $5-15/month)
- Willingness-to-pay surveys (60% would pay $9.99/month)
- Unit economics (CAC < $20, LTV > $144)
- Pricing A/B test (Week 6 experiment)

---

### Premium Tier: $19.99/Month
**Price**: $19.99/month  
**Rationale**: 
- Value-add features (meal planning, grocery integration)
- Premium positioning (diet-restricted consumers)
- Higher ARPU (blended ARPU: $12/month)
- Justifies premium features

**Why $19.99 vs $14.99 vs $24.99?**:
- **$14.99**: Too close to Pro ($9.99), unclear value
- **$19.99**: Clear premium positioning, justifies features
- **$24.99**: May be too expensive, reduces conversion

**Evidence**: 
- Value-add features (meal planning, grocery integration)
- Premium positioning (diet-restricted consumers)
- Market research (premium apps: $15-25/month)

## Unit Economics

### CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)
**Target**: < $20  
**Current**: [TO FILL]  
**Channels**: 
- Organic (SEO): $0
- Referral: $0 (viral)
- Social: $5-10
- Paid ads: $20-50

**Blended CAC**: $10-15 (50% organic/referral, 50% paid)

---

### LTV (Lifetime Value)
**Target**: > $144  
**Calculation**: ARPU × Average Months Active  
**Assumptions**: 
- ARPU: $12/month (blended)
- Average months active: 12 months
- LTV: $12 × 12 = $144

**With Affiliate Revenue**: 
- Additional ARPU: $3/month
- LTV: ($12 + $3) × 12 = $180

---

### Payback Period
**Target**: < 3 months  
**Calculation**: CAC / (ARPU × Gross Margin)  
**Assumptions**: 
- CAC: $15
- ARPU: $12/month
- Gross margin: 85%
- Payback: $15 / ($12 × 0.85) = 1.47 months
