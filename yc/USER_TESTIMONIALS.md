## Testimonial Collection Process

### Step 1: Identify Users

**Sources**:
- Beta users
- Early adopters
- Power users (5+ recipes/week)
- Paying customers
- Users who've been active 30+ days

---

### Step 2: Reach Out

**Email Template**:

```
Subject: Quick Question - Would You Share Your Experience?

Hi [Name],

I noticed you've been using What's for Dinner for [X] days and have generated [Y] recipes. That's awesome!

We're collecting user stories to help other people discover the app. Would you be willing to share a quick quote about your experience?

Just 1-2 sentences about:
- How the app helps you
- What you love about it
- Or any specific benefit you've noticed

If you're open to it, we'd love to feature your quote (with just your first name and location) on our website and in our materials.

Thanks!
[Your Name]
```

---

### Step 3: Follow Up

**If No Response**:
- Send one follow-up after 1 week
- Offer incentive (1 month free, etc.)

**If Yes**:
- Ask for permission to use quote
- Ask for photo (optional)
- Ask for specific metrics (time saved, waste reduced, etc.)

---

### Step 4: Organize

**Store Testimonials**:
- In this document
- In database (if you have one)
- In marketing materials
- On landing page

## Testimonial Usage

### Where to Use

1. **Landing Page**: Hero section, social proof section
2. **App Store**: Screenshots, description
3. **YC Application**: Traction section
4. **Investor Deck**: Social proof slide
5. **Marketing Materials**: Email campaigns, ads

---

### Best Practices

1. **Authenticity**: Use real quotes from real users
2. **Diversity**: Include different user types (families, singles, diet-restricted)
3. **Specificity**: Include metrics when possible (time saved, waste reduced)
4. **Permission**: Always get permission before using
5. **Attribution**: Use first name + location (protect privacy)
