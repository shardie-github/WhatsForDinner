## Storytelling Tips

### For Investors
- **Lead with problem**: Start with the personal problem you faced
- **Show validation**: Demonstrate that others have the same problem
- **Highlight insights**: What do you know that others don't?
- **Demonstrate execution**: Show what you've built, not just what you'll build

### For Users
- **Be authentic**: Share your real story, not a polished version
- **Show empathy**: Demonstrate you understand their pain
- **Build trust**: Show you've solved your own problem first

### For Mentors
- **Be specific**: Use concrete examples, not vague statements
- **Show learning**: Demonstrate how you've learned and iterated
- **Ask for help**: Be honest**: Share failures and learnings, not just wins

## Example Founder Story (Template)

> "I'm [Name], and I built What's for Dinner because I faced the same problem every day: standing in front of my fridge at 6 PM, exhausted from work, wondering what to cook.
>
> I'd waste 15-20 minutes deciding, then end up ordering takeout because I couldn't figure out what to make with what I had. This happened every single day.
>
> I tried recipe sites, meal apps, even ChatGPT - but nothing started with what I actually had in my pantry. So I built a solution for myself: an AI that learns my pantry and suggests recipes based on what I have.
>
> I showed it to friends and family, and they all said the same thing: 'I need this!' That's when I realized this wasn't just my problem - 50 million Americans face the same daily decision fatigue.
>
> We've built the product, validated the problem, and now we're testing willingness to pay. We have [X] users, [Y] recipes generated, and [Z] activation rate. The product is working, and we're ready to scale."
