## Approaches Considered

### Approach 1: Recipe-First (Rejected)
**What**: Start with recipe discovery, then check ingredients  
**Why Considered**: Most recipe sites work this way, familiar pattern  
**Why Rejected**: Doesn't solve decision fatigue (users don't know what they want)  
**Evidence**: User interviews: "Recipe sites are useless - I don't know what I want"

**Status**: ❌ **Rejected**

---

### Approach 2: Meal Planning App (Rejected)
**What**: Weekly meal planning, shopping lists, prep guides  
**Why Considered**: Popular category (Yummly, Mealime)  
**Why Rejected**: Requires planning ahead, doesn't solve "what's for dinner TONIGHT?"  
**Evidence**: User interviews: "Meal apps want me to plan ahead, but I need dinner ideas NOW"

**Status**: ❌ **Rejected**

---

### Approach 3: Generic AI Chatbot (Rejected)
**What**: ChatGPT-style chatbot that suggests recipes  
**Why Considered**: Simple, uses existing AI models  
**Why Rejected**: Doesn't learn preferences, no pantry integration, no personalization  
**Evidence**: User feedback: "ChatGPT can suggest recipes, but it doesn't learn what I like"

**Status**: ❌ **Rejected**

---

### Approach 4: Pantry-First + AI (Chosen)
**What**: Start with pantry, generate personalized recipes with AI  
**Why Chosen**: 
- Solves decision fatigue (starts with what users have)
- AI learns preferences (creates switching cost)
- Pantry integration (reduces waste)
**Evidence**: 
- User feedback: "Finally, an app that starts with what I have!"
- Early validation: 90% prefer pantry-first approach

**Status**: ✅ **Chosen**

---

## Decision Points

### Decision 1: Pantry-First vs Recipe-First
**Options**: Start with pantry vs start with recipes  
**Decision**: Pantry-first  
**Reasoning**: Eliminates decision fatigue, reduces waste  
**Evidence**: User interviews, early feedback

---

### Decision 2: AI Personalization vs Static Recommendations
**Options**: AI that learns vs static recipe database  
**Decision**: AI personalization  
**Reasoning**: Creates switching cost, improves over time  
**Evidence**: Recipe ratings improve over time (learning effect)

---

### Decision 3: Universal Platform vs Single Platform
**Options**: Web + Mobile vs Web only or Mobile only  
**Decision**: Universal platform  
**Reasoning**: Users want to start on phone, finish on tablet  
**Evidence**: Multi-device usage patterns

---

### Decision 4: Subscription vs One-Time Purchase
**Options**: $9.99/month vs $99 one-time  
**Decision**: Subscription  
**Reasoning**: Higher LTV, recurring revenue, better unit economics  
**Evidence**: Market research, competitor analysis

---

### Decision 5: Free Tier vs Paid-Only
**Options**: Free tier with limits vs paid-only  
**Decision**: Free tier (10 recipes/day)  
**Reasoning**: Creates conversion funnel, reduces friction  
**Evidence**: Conversion funnel best practices

## Reasoning Trail

### Why Pantry-First?
**Insight**: Users don't know what they want, they know what they have  
**Evidence**: User interviews, early feedback  
**Decision**: Start with pantry, generate recipes from there

### Why AI Personalization?
**Insight**: Generic recommendations don't create value  
**Evidence**: Recipe ratings improve over time  
**Decision**: AI that learns preferences, creates switching cost

### Why Universal Platform?
**Insight**: Users use multiple devices  
**Evidence**: Multi-device usage patterns  
**Decision**: Web + iOS + Android with seamless sync

### Why Subscription Model?
**Insight**: Recurring revenue > one-time purchase  
**Evidence**: Market research, unit economics  
**Decision**: $9.99/month subscription with free tier
