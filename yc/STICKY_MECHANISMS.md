# Sticky Mechanisms: What's for Dinner

**Purpose**: Document features that make product "sticky" (why users won't switch)  
**Last Updated**: 2025-01-27

---

## Sticky Mechanism 1: Data Moat

### How It Works
**Mechanism**: App learns pantry patterns, preferences, cooking style  
**Switching Cost**: User loses personalization if they switch  
**Impact**: Higher retention, lower churn

### Evidence
- Recipe ratings improve over time (AI learning)
- User satisfaction increases with usage (personalization)
- Retention rate: 40%+ 7-day retention (target)

---

## Sticky Mechanism 2: Habit Formation

### How It Works
**Mechanism**: Daily use creates habit (6 PM dinner decision)  
**Switching Cost**: User needs to form new habit if they switch  
**Impact**: Higher retention, lower churn

### Evidence
- Daily usage pattern (6 PM peak)
- Habit formation (daily recipe generation)
- Retention rate: 40%+ 7-day retention (target)

---

## Sticky Mechanism 3: Network Effects

### How It Works
**Mechanism**: Family sharing → switching requires family to switch too  
**Switching Cost**: User needs to convince family to switch  
**Impact**: Higher retention, lower churn

### Evidence
- Family sharing features (multi-tenant architecture)
- Network effects (family members use app)
- Retention rate: 40%+ 7-day retention (target)
