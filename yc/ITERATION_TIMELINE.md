## Iteration Learnings

### Learning 1: Pantry-First Is The Right Wedge
**What**: Starting with what users have eliminates decision fatigue  
**Evidence**: 90% of users prefer pantry-first approach  
**Impact**: Core differentiator

### Learning 2: AI Personalization Creates Switching Cost
**What**: AI that learns preferences creates moat  
**Evidence**: Recipe ratings improve over time  
**Impact**: Higher retention, lower churn

### Learning 3: Universal Platform Is Differentiator
**What**: Cross-device sync is valuable  
**Evidence**: Users use multiple devices  
**Impact**: Higher engagement, better UX
