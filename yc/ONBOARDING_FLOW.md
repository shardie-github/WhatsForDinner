# Onboarding Flow: What's for Dinner

**Purpose**: Document self-serve journey (signup → activation → engagement)  
**Last Updated**: 2025-01-27

---

## Onboarding Flow Steps

### Step 1: Sign Up
**Goal**: Get user in product  
**Action**: User signs up with email/social login  
**Time**: 30 seconds  
**Success Metric**: 100% completion rate

---

### Step 2: Add Pantry
**Goal**: Create initial value  
**Action**: User adds pantry (scans 3 items)  
**Time**: 2 minutes  
**Success Metric**: 70%+ completion rate

---

### Step 3: Generate First Recipe
**Goal**: Hit "aha moment"  
**Action**: User generates first recipe using pantry items  
**Time**: 30 seconds  
**Success Metric**: 40%+ activation rate

---

### Step 4: Cook and Rate
**Goal**: Create engagement loop  
**Action**: User cooks recipe, rates it (thumbs up/down)  
**Time**: 30 minutes (cooking) + 10 seconds (rating)  
**Success Metric**: 60%+ recipe success rate

---

### Step 5: Invite Family Member
**Goal**: Create viral loop  
**Action**: User invites family member to share meal plans  
**Time**: 1 minute  
**Success Metric**: 30%+ family sharing adoption

---

## Onboarding Success Metrics

### Overall Success
**Metric**: % users who complete all 5 steps  
**Target**: 30%+ (all steps completed)

### Step-by-Step Success
- **Step 1**: 100% completion (signup)
- **Step 2**: 70%+ completion (pantry setup)
- **Step 3**: 40%+ completion (first recipe)
- **Step 4**: 60%+ completion (cook and rate)
- **Step 5**: 30%+ completion (invite family)
