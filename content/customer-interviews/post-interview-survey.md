# Post-Interview Survey

**Purpose**: Gather structured feedback and measure satisfaction after interview  
**Delivery**: Send immediately after interview (within 1 hour)  
**Duration**: 2-3 minutes

---

## Instructions

Hi [Name],

Thank you so much for your time today! Your insights were incredibly valuable.

To help us improve our product and our interview process, I'd love to get your quick feedback on:

1. Your experience with the interview
2. Your initial thoughts on What's for Dinner
3. Your interest in staying involved

This will take just 2-3 minutes. [Link to survey]

## Survey Tools

**Recommended Platforms:**
- Google Forms (free, easy)
- Typeform (better UX, paid)
- SurveyMonkey (paid, advanced analytics)
- Airtable Forms (if using Airtable for tracking)

**Setup:**
1. Create survey in chosen platform
2. Set up email automation to send 1 hour after interview
3. Track responses in spreadsheet/CRM
4. Review weekly with team
