# Vegan Recipes with Pantry Staples: 10 Plant-Based Meals

Eating vegan doesn't have to mean expensive specialty ingredients or complicated recipes. With the right pantry staples, you can create delicious, nutritious plant-based meals that satisfy. Here are 10 vegan recipes using ingredients you probably already have.

## Vegan Pantry Staples

**Essential Vegan Pantry Items:**
- **Beans**: Black, kidney, chickpeas (canned or dried)
- **Grains**: Rice, quinoa, oats, pasta
- **Canned tomatoes**: Diced, crushed, paste
- **Coconut milk**: Full-fat for creaminess
- **Nuts and seeds**: Almonds, walnuts, chia seeds
- **Oils**: Olive oil, coconut oil
- **Spices**: Salt, pepper, garlic powder, Italian seasoning, curry powder

## Quick Vegan Recipes

### 1. Rice and Beans

**Cook time:** 25 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups cooked rice
- 2 cans (15 oz) black beans, drained
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon chili powder
- Salt to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Sauté onion and garlic until soft
2. Add beans, cumin, and chili powder
3. Simmer 10 minutes, season with salt
4. Serve over rice

**Why It's Great:** Complete protein, filling, and flavorful.

### 2. Vegan Pasta with Canned Tomatoes

**Cook time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound pasta
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- Salt and pepper to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Cook pasta according to package directions
2. While pasta cooks, heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Add garlic, cook 1 minute
4. Add tomatoes and seasoning, simmer 10 minutes
5. Toss with pasta, season with salt and pepper

**Why It's Great:** Simple, classic, and satisfying.

### 3. Coconut Curry with Chickpeas

**Cook time:** 25 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups cooked rice
- 1 can (14 oz) coconut milk
- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 tablespoons curry powder
- 1 teaspoon turmeric
- Salt to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Sauté onion until soft
2. Add curry powder and turmeric, cook 1 minute
3. Add coconut milk and chickpeas
4. Simmer 15 minutes
5. Serve over rice

**Why It's Great:** Creamy, flavorful, and protein-rich.

## Hearty Vegan Meals

### 4. Vegan Chili

**Cook time:** 30 minutes | **Serves:** 6

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cans (15 oz) kidney beans
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 can (6 oz) tomato paste
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 tablespoons chili powder
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- Salt to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Sauté onion until soft
2. Add spices, cook 1 minute
3. Add tomatoes, paste, and beans
4. Simmer 20 minutes
5. Season with salt

**Why It's Great:** Hearty, filling, and perfect for meal prep.

### 5. Vegan Minestrone Soup

**Cook time:** 35 minutes | **Serves:** 6

**Ingredients:**
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 can (15 oz) kidney beans
- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas
- 1 cup pasta
- 4 cups vegetable broth
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- Salt and pepper to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Sauté onion until soft
2. Add tomatoes, beans, and broth
3. Bring to boil, add pasta
4. Simmer 15 minutes until pasta is tender
5. Season with salt and pepper

**Why It's Great:** Comforting, nutritious, and perfect for cold days.

### 6. Peanut Butter Noodles

**Cook time:** 15 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound pasta
- 1/4 cup peanut butter
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon vinegar
- 1 tablespoon maple syrup or sugar
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- Red pepper flakes (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Cook pasta according to package
2. Mix peanut butter, soy sauce, vinegar, and maple syrup
3. Add garlic and red pepper flakes
4. Toss with pasta
5. Add pasta water if needed to thin sauce

**Why It's Great:** Quick, creamy, and satisfying.

## Vegan Breakfast Options

### 7. Overnight Oats

**Prep time:** 5 minutes | **Serves:** 1

**Ingredients:**
- 1/2 cup oats
- 1/2 cup plant-based milk (almond, soy, oat)
- 2 tablespoons peanut butter
- 1 tablespoon maple syrup
- 1/4 cup berries (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Mix all ingredients
2. Refrigerate overnight
3. Enjoy in the morning
4. Top with more berries if desired

**Why It's Great:** Perfect for busy mornings, nutritious, and filling.

### 8. Chia Pudding

**Prep time:** 5 minutes (+ overnight) | **Serves:** 2

**Ingredients:**
- 1/4 cup chia seeds
- 1 cup plant-based milk
- 1 tablespoon maple syrup
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Fresh fruit (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Mix all ingredients
2. Refrigerate overnight
3. Stir before serving
4. Top with fresh fruit

**Why It's Great:** High in omega-3s, protein, and fiber.

## Vegan Snacks

### 9. Roasted Chickpeas

**Cook time:** 30 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cans (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 400°F
2. Pat chickpeas dry
3. Toss with oil and spices
4. Spread on baking sheet
5. Roast 25-30 minutes until crispy

**Why It's Great:** High in protein, crispy, and addictive.

### 10. Vegan Granola Bars

**Cook time:** 25 minutes | **Makes:** 12 bars

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups oats
- 1/2 cup peanut butter
- 1/3 cup maple syrup
- 1/2 cup nuts (almonds, walnuts)
- 1/4 cup dried fruit
- Pinch of salt

**Instructions:**
1. Mix all ingredients
2. Press into baking dish
3. Bake 350°F for 20 minutes
4. Cool completely before cutting

**Why It's Great:** Perfect for snacks, meal prep, and on-the-go.

- Nutritional yeast
- Dried fruits
- Canned vegetables

## Tips for Vegan Pantry Cooking

1. **Stock up on beans**: High in protein and versatile
2. **Use coconut milk**: Adds creaminess to sauces and curries
3. **Spices are key**: Make simple ingredients flavorful
4. **Plan for protein**: Include beans, nuts, or seeds in every meal
5. **Keep it simple**: Fewer ingredients often taste better

## Common Vegan Substitutions

- **Milk**: Plant-based milk (almond, soy, oat)
- **Butter**: Olive oil, coconut oil, or vegan butter
- **Cheese**: Nutritional yeast, vegan cheese, or omit
- **Eggs**: Chia seeds, flax seeds, or applesauce (for baking)
- **Honey**: Maple syrup, agave, or sugar
