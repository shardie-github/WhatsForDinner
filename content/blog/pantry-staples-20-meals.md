# 20 Pantry Staples Recipes: Make Delicious Meals with What You Have

Ever look at your pantry and think, "What can I make with all this?" You're not alone. Most pantries are filled with amazing ingredients just waiting to become delicious meals. Here are 20 recipes you can make right now using common pantry staples.

## Why Pantry-First Cooking Works

Cooking with pantry staples saves time, money, and reduces food waste. Instead of running to the store for specific ingredients, you can create satisfying meals with what you already have.

## Tips for Pantry-First Cooking

1. **Keep Stocked:** Maintain a well-stocked pantry with staples like rice, pasta, canned beans, tomatoes, and spices.

2. **Be Creative:** Don't be afraid to substitute ingredients - most recipes are flexible.

3. **Plan Ahead:** Use a meal planning app to generate recipes based on your pantry items.

4. **Rotate Stock:** Use older items first to prevent waste.

5. **Think Versatile:** Choose ingredients that work in multiple recipes.
