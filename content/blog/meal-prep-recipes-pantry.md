# Meal Prep Recipes That Use What You Have: 10 Make-Ahead Meals

Meal prep doesn't have to mean expensive grocery trips. With pantry staples, you can create delicious make-ahead meals that save time and money. Here are 10 meal prep recipes using ingredients you probably already have.

## Why Pantry Meal Prep Works

Meal prepping with pantry staples is smart because:
- **Saves money**: Use what you have, no waste
- **Saves time**: Prep once, eat all week
- **Reduces stress**: No daily "what's for dinner?" decisions
- **Portion control**: Perfect for weight management
- **Flexible**: Easy to adapt to what you have

## Make-Ahead Breakfasts

### 1. Overnight Oats (5 Variations)

**Prep time:** 5 minutes | **Serves:** 1 per batch

**Base Recipe:**
- 1/2 cup oats
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1 tablespoon sweetener (honey, maple syrup, or sugar)
- Pinch of salt

**Variations:**
- **Peanut Butter Banana**: Add 2 tablespoons peanut butter, 1 banana
- **Apple Cinnamon**: Add 1/2 apple, 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
- **Chocolate**: Add 1 tablespoon cocoa powder, 1 tablespoon chocolate chips
- **Berry**: Add 1/2 cup berries, 1/2 teaspoon vanilla
- **Maple Pecan**: Add 1 tablespoon maple syrup, 1/4 cup pecans

**Instructions:**
1. Mix all ingredients
2. Refrigerate overnight
3. Enjoy cold or heat in the morning

**Storage:** 5 days in refrigerator

### 2. Make-Ahead Breakfast Burritos

**Prep time:** 30 minutes | **Serves:** 8

**Ingredients:**
- 8 eggs
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans
- 1 cup salsa
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- 8 tortillas
- Salt and pepper

**Instructions:**
1. Scramble eggs, season with salt and pepper
2. Heat beans
3. Assemble burritos: eggs, beans, salsa, cheese
4. Wrap tightly
5. Freeze individually
6. Reheat in microwave 2-3 minutes

**Storage:** 3 months in freezer

## Lunch Meal Prep

### 3. Quinoa Salad Jars

**Prep time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups cooked quinoa
- 2 cans (15 oz) black beans, drained
- 1 can (14 oz) corn, drained
- 1 cup salsa
- 1/2 cup shredded cheese
- 4 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 tablespoons lime juice

**Instructions:**
1. Layer quinoa in jars
2. Add beans and corn
3. Top with salsa and cheese
4. Mix olive oil and lime juice, pour over
5. Seal and refrigerate

**Storage:** 5 days in refrigerator

### 4. Tuna Salad Meal Prep

**Prep time:** 15 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 4 cans (5 oz) tuna, drained
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1/4 cup pickles, chopped
- 1/4 cup celery (if available)
- 1 tablespoon mustard
- Salt and pepper

**Instructions:**
1. Mix all ingredients
2. Divide into 4 containers
3. Serve with crackers or on bread
4. Store separately from bread/crackers

**Storage:** 5 days in refrigerator

### 5. Rice and Beans Meal Prep

**Prep time:** 30 minutes | **Serves:** 6

**Ingredients:**
- 3 cups cooked rice
- 3 cans (15 oz) black beans
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon chili powder
- Salt to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Sauté onion and garlic
2. Add beans, tomatoes, and spices
3. Simmer 15 minutes
4. Divide rice and beans into containers
5. Store separately or together

**Storage:** 5 days in refrigerator

## Dinner Meal Prep

### 6. Pantry Chili Meal Prep

**Prep time:** 40 minutes | **Serves:** 8

**Ingredients:**
- 2 pounds ground beef
- 2 cans (15 oz) kidney beans
- 2 cans (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 can (6 oz) tomato paste
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 tablespoons chili powder
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- Salt to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Brown ground beef
2. Add onion, cook until soft
3. Add spices, cook 1 minute
4. Add tomatoes, paste, and beans
5. Simmer 20 minutes
6. Divide into containers

**Storage:** 5 days in refrigerator, 3 months in freezer

### 7. Coconut Curry Meal Prep

**Prep time:** 30 minutes | **Serves:** 6

**Ingredients:**
- 3 cups cooked rice
- 2 cans (14 oz) coconut milk
- 2 cans (15 oz) chickpeas
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 tablespoons curry powder
- 1 teaspoon turmeric
- Salt to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Sauté onion
2. Add curry powder and turmeric
3. Add coconut milk and chickpeas
4. Simmer 15 minutes
5. Divide rice and curry into containers

**Storage:** 5 days in refrigerator

### 8. Pasta Salad Meal Prep

**Prep time:** 25 minutes | **Serves:** 6

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound pasta
- 1 can (14 oz) tuna, drained
- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 2 tablespoons vinegar
- Salt and pepper

**Instructions:**
1. Cook pasta, cool
2. Mix mayonnaise, oil, and vinegar
3. Toss with pasta, tuna, and chickpeas
4. Season with salt and pepper
5. Divide into containers

**Storage:** 5 days in refrigerator

## Snack Meal Prep

### 9. Granola Bars

**Prep time:** 15 minutes | **Makes:** 12 bars

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups oats
- 1/2 cup peanut butter
- 1/3 cup honey
- 1/2 cup nuts (optional)
- 1/4 cup dried fruit (optional)
- Pinch of salt

**Instructions:**
1. Mix all ingredients
2. Press into baking dish
3. Bake 350°F for 20 minutes
4. Cool completely
5. Cut into bars
6. Store individually

**Storage:** 2 weeks at room temperature, 3 months in freezer

### 10. Trail Mix

**Prep time:** 5 minutes | **Serves:** 8

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups mixed nuts
- 1 cup dried fruit
- 1/2 cup seeds
- 1/2 cup chocolate chips (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Mix all ingredients
2. Divide into portions
3. Store in airtight containers

**Storage:** 1 month at room temperature

## Meal Prep Tips

1. **Invest in containers**: Good containers make meal prep easier
2. **Label everything**: Date and contents on each container
3. **Prep in batches**: Cook rice, beans, etc. in large batches
4. **Keep it simple**: Don't overcomplicate
5. **Plan your week**: Know what you're making each day

## Meal Prep Storage Guidelines

**Refrigerator (5 days max):**
- Cooked grains (rice, quinoa)
- Cooked beans
- Soups and stews
- Salads with dressing

**Freezer (3 months max):**
- Soups and stews
- Casseroles
- Breakfast burritos
- Chili

**Room Temperature (1-2 weeks):**
- Granola bars
- Trail mix
- Crackers
- Bread

## Meal Prep Planning

**Weekly Schedule:**
- **Sunday**: Prep for the week
- **Wednesday**: Mid-week refresh (optional)
- **Friday**: Use up leftovers

**Time-Saving Tips:**
- Cook rice and beans in large batches
- Pre-portion snacks
- Use slow cooker for hands-off cooking
- Prep vegetables in advance
