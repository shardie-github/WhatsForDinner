# What to Make with Chicken and Rice: 15 Easy Recipes You Can Make Tonight

Staring at chicken and rice in your pantry and wondering what to make? You're not alone. These two ingredients are the foundation of countless delicious meals. Whether you're looking for a quick weeknight dinner or something more adventurous, we've got you covered.

## Why Chicken and Rice?

Chicken and rice are the perfect pantry staples because they're:
- **Affordable**: Both ingredients are budget-friendly
- **Versatile**: Work in countless cuisines and cooking styles
- **Nutritious**: Complete protein and complex carbs
- **Quick to cook**: Most recipes come together in 30 minutes or less

## Quick and Easy Recipes

### 1. One-Pan Chicken and Rice

**Cook time:** 30 minutes | **Serves:** 4

This is the ultimate lazy dinner. Everything cooks in one pan, so cleanup is minimal.

**Ingredients:**
- 4 chicken thighs
- 1 cup white rice
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- Salt and pepper to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Season chicken thighs with salt and pepper
2. Brown chicken in a large skillet, remove
3. Sauté onion and garlic in the same pan
4. Add rice and broth, bring to boil
5. Return chicken to pan, cover and simmer 20 minutes
6. Let rest 5 minutes before serving

### 2. Chicken Fried Rice

**Cook time:** 15 minutes | **Serves:** 4

Transform leftover rice into a restaurant-quality meal.

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups cooked rice (day-old works best)
- 2 chicken breasts, diced
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup frozen vegetables
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 2 green onions, sliced

**Instructions:**
1. Scramble eggs in a large wok, remove
2. Cook chicken until golden, remove
3. Add vegetables, cook until tender
4. Add rice, break up any clumps
5. Return eggs and chicken
6. Season with soy sauce and sesame oil
7. Garnish with green onions

### 3. Chicken and Rice Casserole

**Cook time:** 45 minutes | **Serves:** 6

Comfort food at its finest. Perfect for meal prep.

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups cooked rice
- 2 cups cooked, shredded chicken
- 1 can cream of chicken soup
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- 1/2 cup breadcrumbs

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 375°F
2. Mix rice, chicken, soup, and sour cream
3. Transfer to baking dish
4. Top with cheese and breadcrumbs
5. Bake 25-30 minutes until golden

### 4. Arroz con Pollo (Chicken and Rice)

**Cook time:** 40 minutes | **Serves:** 6

A Latin American classic that's easier than you think.

**Ingredients:**
- 6 chicken thighs
- 2 cups rice
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 4 cups chicken broth
- Saffron or turmeric (optional, for color)

**Instructions:**
1. Season and brown chicken, remove
2. Sauté vegetables until soft
3. Add rice, spices, and tomatoes
4. Pour in broth, bring to boil
5. Return chicken, cover and simmer 25 minutes
6. Let rest 10 minutes before serving

## Tips for Perfect Chicken and Rice

1. **Use the right rice**: Long-grain for fluffy, short-grain for sticky
2. **Don't skip the rest**: Let rice rest 5-10 minutes after cooking
3. **Season generously**: Both chicken and rice need seasoning
4. **Use broth**: Replace water with broth for more flavor
5. **Don't peek**: Keep lid on while rice cooks

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I use brown rice instead of white?**
A: Yes! Adjust cooking time and liquid ratio (typically 1:2 rice to liquid, 45 minutes cook time).

**Q: What if I only have chicken breasts?**
A: Most recipes work with any cut. Adjust cooking time - breasts cook faster than thighs.

**Q: Can I make these recipes ahead?**
A: Absolutely! Many of these recipes are great for meal prep and reheat well.
