# 30-Minute Recipes with 5 Ingredients: 12 Fast & Easy Meals

When time is tight, you need recipes that deliver big flavor with minimal effort. These 12 recipes use just 5 ingredients each (plus salt, pepper, and oil) and come together in 30 minutes or less. Perfect for busy weeknights.

## The 30-Minute Formula

**Successful 30-minute recipes need:**
- Simple techniques (sauté, bake, simmer)
- Few ingredients (less chopping, less prep)
- Quick cooking proteins (chicken, fish, ground meat)
- Pre-made shortcuts (jarred sauces, canned goods)
- One-pan meals (less cleanup)

## Super Fast (15-20 Minutes)

### 1. Lemon Garlic Chicken

**Cook time:** 15 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 4 chicken breasts
- 4 cloves garlic
- 2 lemons
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Fresh parsley (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Season chicken with salt and pepper
2. Heat oil in large skillet
3. Cook chicken 6-7 minutes per side
4. Add garlic, cook 1 minute
5. Add lemon juice, simmer 2 minutes
6. Garnish with parsley

### 2. 5-Ingredient Pasta

**Cook time:** 15 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound pasta
- 1 jar marinara sauce
- 1 pound ground beef
- 1 onion
- Parmesan cheese

**Instructions:**
1. Cook pasta according to package
2. Brown beef with onion
3. Add marinara, simmer 5 minutes
4. Toss with pasta
5. Top with Parmesan

### 3. Salmon with Soy Glaze

**Cook time:** 15 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 4 salmon fillets
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons honey
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tablespoon oil

**Instructions:**
1. Mix soy sauce, honey, and garlic
2. Heat oil in skillet
3. Cook salmon 4-5 minutes per side
4. Pour glaze over salmon
5. Simmer 2 minutes until glazed

## One-Pan Meals (20-25 Minutes)

### 4. Sheet Pan Chicken and Vegetables

**Cook time:** 25 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 4 chicken thighs
- 1 pound baby potatoes
- 2 bell peppers
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Italian seasoning

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 425°F
2. Toss everything with oil and seasoning
3. Arrange on sheet pan
4. Bake 25 minutes until chicken is cooked

### 5. 5-Ingredient Stir Fry

**Cook time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound chicken, sliced
- 1 bag frozen vegetables
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons sesame oil
- 2 cups cooked rice

**Instructions:**
1. Heat sesame oil in wok
2. Cook chicken until done
3. Add vegetables, cook until tender
4. Add soy sauce, toss
5. Serve over rice

### 6. Sausage and Peppers

**Cook time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound Italian sausage
- 2 bell peppers
- 1 onion
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 2 tablespoons olive oil

**Instructions:**
1. Brown sausage, remove
2. Sauté peppers and onion
3. Add tomatoes and sausage
4. Simmer 10 minutes
5. Serve hot

## Comfort Food Fast (25-30 Minutes)

### 7. 5-Ingredient Mac and Cheese

**Cook time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound pasta
- 2 cups shredded cheddar
- 2 cups milk
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 tablespoons flour

**Instructions:**
1. Cook pasta according to package
2. Melt butter, whisk in flour
3. Add milk, whisk until smooth
4. Add cheese, stir until melted
5. Toss with pasta

### 8. Simple Beef Tacos

**Cook time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound ground beef
- 1 packet taco seasoning
- 8 taco shells
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- Sour cream (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Brown beef, drain fat
2. Add taco seasoning and water
3. Simmer 5 minutes
4. Fill taco shells
5. Top with cheese and sour cream

### 9. Chicken and Rice Casserole

**Cook time:** 30 minutes | **Serves:** 6

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups cooked rice
- 2 cups cooked, shredded chicken
- 1 can cream of chicken soup
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- 1/2 cup breadcrumbs

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 375°F
2. Mix rice, chicken, and soup
3. Transfer to baking dish
4. Top with cheese and breadcrumbs
5. Bake 25 minutes

## Healthy & Fast (20-30 Minutes)

### 10. Greek Chicken Salad

**Cook time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 4 chicken breasts
- 1 head romaine lettuce
- 1 cup feta cheese
- 1/2 cup Greek dressing
- 1/2 cup Kalamata olives

**Instructions:**
1. Season and cook chicken
2. Slice chicken
3. Toss lettuce with dressing
4. Top with chicken, feta, and olives

### 11. Quinoa Bowl

**Cook time:** 25 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups cooked quinoa
- 1 can black beans
- 1 avocado
- 1 cup salsa
- 1/2 cup shredded cheese

**Instructions:**
1. Divide quinoa among bowls
2. Top with beans
3. Add avocado and salsa
4. Top with cheese

### 12. Baked Salmon and Asparagus

**Cook time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 4 salmon fillets
- 1 pound asparagus
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 lemons
- Fresh dill (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 400°F
2. Arrange salmon and asparagus on sheet pan
3. Drizzle with oil and lemon juice
4. Season with salt and pepper
5. Bake 15-18 minutes
6. Garnish with dill

## Time-Saving Tips

1. **Prep ahead**: Chop vegetables the night before
2. **Use shortcuts**: Pre-cooked proteins, jarred sauces
3. **One-pan meals**: Less cleanup
4. **Keep it simple**: Fewer ingredients = faster cooking
5. **Batch cook**: Make extra for leftovers

## 30-Minute Success Secrets

1. **Read recipe first**: Know what you're doing
2. **Prep ingredients**: Have everything ready
3. **Start with longest-cooking item**: Get rice/water boiling first
4. **Clean as you go**: Save time later
5. **Don't overthink**: Simple is better
