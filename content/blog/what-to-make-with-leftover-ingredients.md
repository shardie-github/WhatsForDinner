# What to Make with Leftover Ingredients: 15 Creative Recipes

Leftovers are a goldmine for creative cooking. Instead of letting leftover ingredients go to waste, transform them into delicious new meals. Here are 15 creative recipes using leftover ingredients.

## Why Use Leftovers?

### Benefits
- **Reduce Food Waste**: Use ingredients before they expire
- **Save Money**: Use what you have, buy less
- **Save Time**: No shopping required
- **Creative Cooking**: Transform leftovers into new meals

## Leftover Chicken Recipes

### 1. Chicken Salad
**Ingredients**: Leftover chicken, mayonnaise, celery, onion
**Time**: 10 minutes

Mix shredded chicken with mayonnaise, diced celery, and onion. Serve on bread or in a wrap.

### 2. Chicken Soup
**Ingredients**: Leftover chicken, chicken broth, vegetables, noodles
**Time**: 30 minutes

Sauté vegetables, add broth and chicken, simmer, add noodles.

### 3. Chicken Tacos
**Ingredients**: Leftover chicken, tortillas, salsa, cheese
**Time**: 15 minutes

Shred chicken, warm tortillas, fill with chicken and toppings.

## Leftover Rice Recipes

### 4. Fried Rice
**Ingredients**: Leftover rice, vegetables, protein, soy sauce
**Time**: 15 minutes

Sauté vegetables and protein, add rice, stir-fry with soy sauce.

### 5. Rice Pudding
**Ingredients**: Leftover rice, milk, sugar, vanilla
**Time**: 30 minutes

Simmer rice with milk and sugar, add vanilla, serve warm or cold.

### 6. Rice Bowls
**Ingredients**: Leftover rice, vegetables, protein, sauce
**Time**: 15 minutes

Top rice with vegetables, protein, and sauce.

## Leftover Vegetable Recipes

### 7. Vegetable Stir-Fry
**Ingredients**: Leftover vegetables, protein, soy sauce
**Time**: 15 minutes

Sauté vegetables and protein, add soy sauce, serve over rice.

### 8. Vegetable Soup
**Ingredients**: Leftover vegetables, broth, herbs
**Time**: 30 minutes

Sauté vegetables, add broth, simmer, add herbs.

### 9. Vegetable Frittata
**Ingredients**: Leftover vegetables, eggs, cheese
**Time**: 25 minutes

Sauté vegetables, add beaten eggs, bake in oven.

## Leftover Pasta Recipes

### 10. Pasta Salad
**Ingredients**: Leftover pasta, vegetables, dressing
**Time**: 10 minutes

Mix pasta with vegetables and dressing, chill.

### 11. Pasta Frittata
**Ingredients**: Leftover pasta, eggs, cheese
**Time**: 25 minutes

Mix pasta with beaten eggs, bake in oven.

### 12. Pasta Soup
**Ingredients**: Leftover pasta, broth, vegetables
**Time**: 20 minutes

Heat broth, add pasta and vegetables, serve.

## Leftover Bread Recipes

### 13. Bread Pudding
**Ingredients**: Leftover bread, milk, eggs, sugar
**Time**: 45 minutes

Mix bread with milk, eggs, and sugar, bake.

### 14. Croutons
**Ingredients**: Leftover bread, olive oil, herbs
**Time**: 15 minutes

Cut bread into cubes, toss with oil and herbs, bake.

### 15. French Toast
**Ingredients**: Leftover bread, eggs, milk, cinnamon
**Time**: 15 minutes

Dip bread in egg mixture, cook in pan.

## Tips for Using Leftovers

### 1. Store Properly
Store leftovers in airtight containers in the fridge or freezer.

### 2. Use Within 3-4 Days
Use leftovers within 3-4 days for best quality.

### 3. Transform, Don't Repeat
Transform leftovers into new meals, don't just reheat.

### 4. Freeze for Later
Freeze leftovers if you won't use them soon.

### 5. Plan Leftover Meals
Plan meals around leftovers to reduce waste.
