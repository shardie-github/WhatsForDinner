# Budget-Friendly Recipes from Pantry: 12 Meals Under $5

Eating well doesn't have to break the bank. With smart pantry shopping and these budget-friendly recipes, you can create delicious, satisfying meals for less than $5 per serving. Here are 12 recipes that prove cheap can be delicious.

## Budget Pantry Basics

**Best Value Pantry Items:**
- **Rice**: $0.10 per serving
- **Pasta**: $0.15 per serving
- **Beans**: $0.20 per serving
- **Canned tomatoes**: $0.30 per serving
- **Eggs**: $0.25 per serving
- **Canned tuna**: $0.50 per serving

## Super Budget Meals (Under $2 per serving)

### 1. Rice and Beans

**Cost:** $1.50 per serving | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups rice ($0.40)
- 2 cans black beans ($1.60)
- 1 onion ($0.30)
- 2 cloves garlic ($0.10)
- Spices ($0.20)

**Total:** $2.60 for 4 servings

**Instructions:**
1. Cook rice
2. Sauté onion and garlic
3. Add beans and spices
4. Simmer 10 minutes
5. Serve over rice

### 2. Pasta with Canned Tomatoes

**Cost:** $1.75 per serving | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound pasta ($1.00)
- 1 can diced tomatoes ($1.00)
- 2 cloves garlic ($0.10)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil ($0.20)
- Spices ($0.20)

**Total:** $2.50 for 4 servings

**Instructions:**
1. Cook pasta
2. Sauté garlic
3. Add tomatoes and spices
4. Simmer 10 minutes
5. Toss with pasta

### 3. Peanut Butter Noodles

**Cost:** $1.50 per serving | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound pasta ($1.00)
- 1/4 cup peanut butter ($0.30)
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce ($0.10)
- 1 tablespoon vinegar ($0.05)
- 2 cloves garlic ($0.10)

**Total:** $1.55 for 4 servings

**Instructions:**
1. Cook pasta
2. Mix peanut butter, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic
3. Toss with pasta
4. Add pasta water if needed

## Hearty Budget Meals ($2-3 per serving)

### 4. Pantry Chili

**Cost:** $2.50 per serving | **Serves:** 6

**Ingredients:**
- 2 pounds ground beef ($8.00)
- 2 cans kidney beans ($1.60)
- 1 can diced tomatoes ($1.00)
- 1 can tomato paste ($0.50)
- 1 onion ($0.30)
- Spices ($0.30)

**Total:** $11.70 for 6 servings

**Instructions:**
1. Brown ground beef
2. Add onion and spices
3. Add tomatoes, paste, and beans
4. Simmer 20 minutes

### 5. Tuna Pasta Salad

**Cost:** $2.25 per serving | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound pasta ($1.00)
- 2 cans tuna ($2.00)
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise ($0.50)
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice ($0.05)
- Spices ($0.10)

**Total:** $3.65 for 4 servings

**Instructions:**
1. Cook and cool pasta
2. Mix tuna with mayonnaise and lemon
3. Toss with pasta
4. Season to taste

### 6. Coconut Curry with Rice

**Cost:** $2.75 per serving | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups rice ($0.40)
- 1 can coconut milk ($1.50)
- 1 can chickpeas ($0.80)
- 1 onion ($0.30)
- Spices ($0.30)

**Total:** $3.30 for 4 servings

**Instructions:**
1. Cook rice
2. Sauté onion
3. Add spices
4. Add coconut milk and chickpeas
5. Simmer 15 minutes
6. Serve over rice

## Budget-Friendly Comfort Food ($3-4 per serving)

### 7. Chicken and Rice Casserole

**Cost:** $3.50 per serving | **Serves:** 6

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups rice ($0.40)
- 2 cans chicken ($4.00)
- 1 can cream of chicken soup ($1.00)
- 1 cup shredded cheese ($1.50)
- Breadcrumbs ($0.30)

**Total:** $7.20 for 6 servings

**Instructions:**
1. Cook rice
2. Mix rice, chicken, and soup
3. Transfer to baking dish
4. Top with cheese and breadcrumbs
5. Bake 25 minutes

### 8. Pantry Minestrone Soup

**Cost:** $3.00 per serving | **Serves:** 6

**Ingredients:**
- 1 can diced tomatoes ($1.00)
- 2 cans beans ($1.60)
- 1 cup pasta ($0.25)
- 4 cups broth ($1.00)
- 1 onion ($0.30)
- Spices ($0.20)

**Total:** $4.35 for 6 servings

**Instructions:**
1. Sauté onion
2. Add tomatoes, beans, and broth
3. Bring to boil
4. Add pasta
5. Simmer 15 minutes

### 9. Pantry Fried Rice

**Cost:** $2.50 per serving | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 3 cups cooked rice ($0.60)
- 2 eggs ($0.50)
- 1 can mixed vegetables ($0.80)
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce ($0.10)
- 1 tablespoon oil ($0.10)

**Total:** $2.10 for 4 servings

**Instructions:**
1. Scramble eggs, remove
2. Add vegetables
3. Add rice
4. Return eggs
5. Add soy sauce

## Budget Breakfast Options

### 10. Overnight Oats

**Cost:** $1.00 per serving | **Serves:** 1

**Ingredients:**
- 1/2 cup oats ($0.15)
- 1/2 cup milk ($0.20)
- 2 tablespoons peanut butter ($0.15)
- 1 tablespoon honey ($0.10)
- Pinch of salt ($0.01)

**Total:** $0.61 per serving

**Instructions:**
1. Mix all ingredients
2. Refrigerate overnight
3. Enjoy in the morning

### 11. Simple Pancakes

**Cost:** $1.25 per serving | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups flour ($0.30)
- 2 eggs ($0.50)
- 2 cups milk ($0.80)
- 2 tablespoons oil ($0.20)
- 2 teaspoons baking powder ($0.10)

**Total:** $1.90 for 4 servings

**Instructions:**
1. Mix dry ingredients
2. Mix wet ingredients
3. Combine
4. Cook on griddle
5. Serve with syrup

### 12. Granola Bars

**Cost:** $1.50 per serving | **Makes:** 12 bars

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups oats ($0.60)
- 1/2 cup peanut butter ($0.60)
- 1/3 cup honey ($0.50)
- 1/2 cup nuts ($1.00)
- 1/4 cup dried fruit ($0.50)

**Total:** $3.20 for 12 bars

**Instructions:**
1. Mix all ingredients
2. Press into baking dish
3. Bake 350°F for 20 minutes
4. Cool and cut

## Budget Shopping Tips

1. **Buy in bulk**: Larger packages cost less per serving
2. **Shop sales**: Stock up on sale items
3. **Use coupons**: Clip coupons for pantry staples
4. **Compare prices**: Check unit prices, not package prices
5. **Buy store brands**: Often same quality, lower price
6. **Plan meals**: Reduce waste and impulse purchases
