# One-Pan Pantry Meals: 10 Easy Recipes with Minimal Cleanup

One-pan meals are the secret weapon of busy cooks. They're quick, require minimal cleanup, and deliver big flavor. These 10 pantry-based one-pan meals come together in 30 minutes or less and use ingredients you probably already have.

## Why One-Pan Meals Work

**Benefits:**
- **Less cleanup**: One pan instead of multiple pots
- **Faster cooking**: Everything cooks together
- **Better flavor**: Ingredients cook together, flavors meld
- **Less stress**: Simpler preparation
- **Perfect for meal prep**: Easy to scale up

## Sheet Pan Meals

### 1. Sheet Pan Chicken and Vegetables

**Cook time:** 25 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 4 chicken thighs
- 1 pound baby potatoes
- 2 bell peppers, sliced
- 1 onion, sliced
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Italian seasoning
- Salt and pepper

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 425°F
2. Toss everything with oil and seasoning
3. Arrange on sheet pan
4. Bake 25 minutes until chicken is cooked
5. Serve hot

### 2. Sheet Pan Salmon and Asparagus

**Cook time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 4 salmon fillets
- 1 pound asparagus
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 lemons, sliced
- Salt and pepper

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 400°F
2. Arrange salmon and asparagus on sheet pan
3. Drizzle with oil
4. Season with salt and pepper
5. Top with lemon slices
6. Bake 15-18 minutes

### 3. Sheet Pan Sausage and Vegetables

**Cook time:** 25 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound Italian sausage
- 1 pound baby potatoes
- 2 bell peppers, sliced
- 1 onion, sliced
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Italian seasoning

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 425°F
2. Toss everything with oil and seasoning
3. Arrange on sheet pan
4. Bake 25 minutes until sausage is cooked
5. Serve hot

## Skillet Meals

### 4. One-Pan Chicken and Rice

**Cook time:** 30 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 4 chicken thighs
- 1 cup white rice
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- Salt and pepper

**Instructions:**
1. Season chicken with salt and pepper
2. Brown chicken in large skillet, remove
3. Sauté onion and garlic
4. Add rice and broth, bring to boil
5. Return chicken to pan
6. Cover and simmer 20 minutes
7. Let rest 5 minutes before serving

### 5. One-Pan Pasta

**Cook time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound pasta
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 4 cups water
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Italian seasoning

**Instructions:**
1. Combine all ingredients in large skillet
2. Bring to boil
3. Simmer 15 minutes, stirring occasionally
4. Cook until pasta is tender and liquid is absorbed
5. Serve hot

### 6. One-Pan Taco Skillet

**Cook time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound ground beef
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 cup corn (canned or frozen)
- 1 packet taco seasoning
- 1 cup shredded cheese

**Instructions:**
1. Brown ground beef
2. Add taco seasoning
3. Add beans, tomatoes, and corn
4. Simmer 10 minutes
5. Top with cheese
6. Serve hot

## Casserole-Style One-Pan Meals

### 7. One-Pan Chicken and Rice Casserole

**Cook time:** 30 minutes | **Serves:** 6

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups cooked rice
- 2 cups cooked, shredded chicken
- 1 can cream of chicken soup
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
- 2 tablespoons butter

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 375°F
2. Mix rice, chicken, and soup in baking dish
3. Top with cheese and breadcrumbs
4. Dot with butter
5. Bake 25 minutes until golden

### 8. One-Pan Baked Ziti

**Cook time:** 30 minutes | **Serves:** 6

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound pasta
- 1 jar marinara sauce
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
- 2 cups water

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 375°F
2. Combine pasta, sauce, and water in baking dish
3. Cover and bake 20 minutes
4. Uncover, top with cheeses
5. Bake 10 minutes until bubbly

## One-Pan Breakfasts

### 9. One-Pan Breakfast Hash

**Cook time:** 25 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound potatoes, diced
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 4 eggs
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper

**Instructions:**
1. Heat oil in large skillet
2. Add potatoes, cook 15 minutes
3. Add onion and pepper
4. Cook 5 minutes
5. Make 4 wells in hash
6. Crack eggs into wells
7. Cover and cook 5 minutes until eggs are set

### 10. One-Pan Pancakes

**Cook time:** 15 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups flour
- 2 eggs
- 2 cups milk
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 2 teaspoons baking powder

**Instructions:**
1. Mix all ingredients
2. Heat large skillet
3. Pour batter in circles
4. Cook until bubbles form
5. Flip and cook other side
6. Repeat with remaining batter

## Tips for One-Pan Success

1. **Choose the right pan**: Large enough for all ingredients
2. **Preheat properly**: Ensures even cooking
3. **Cut uniform sizes**: Everything cooks at the same rate
4. **Don't overcrowd**: Give ingredients space
5. **Stir occasionally**: Ensures even cooking

## One-Pan Meal Prep

**Make ahead:**
- Chop vegetables the night before
- Pre-cook rice or pasta
- Marinate proteins in advance

**Store:**
- Refrigerate up to 5 days
- Reheat in oven or microwave
- Freeze for up to 3 months
