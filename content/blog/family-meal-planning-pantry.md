# Family Meal Planning with Pantry Staples: 7-Day Meal Plan

Planning family meals doesn't have to be stressful or expensive. This complete 7-day meal plan uses pantry staples and feeds a family of 4 for less than $75. Includes breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks for the entire week.

## Meal Planning Basics

**Why Meal Planning Works:**
- **Saves money**: Buy only what you need
- **Saves time**: No daily "what's for dinner?" decisions
- **Reduces stress**: Plan once, eat all week
- **Less waste**: Use everything you buy
- **Better nutrition**: Plan balanced meals

## 7-Day Meal Plan

### Monday

**Breakfast:** Overnight Oats
- 1/2 cup oats, 1/2 cup milk, 2 tablespoons peanut butter, 1 tablespoon honey

**Lunch:** Tuna Salad Sandwiches
- 2 cans tuna, 1/2 cup mayonnaise, 8 slices bread

**Dinner:** Chicken and Rice Casserole
- 2 cups rice, 2 cups cooked chicken, 1 can cream of chicken soup, 1 cup cheese

**Snack:** Granola Bars
- 2 cups oats, 1/2 cup peanut butter, 1/3 cup honey

### Tuesday

**Breakfast:** Pancakes
- 2 cups flour, 2 eggs, 2 cups milk, 2 tablespoons oil

**Lunch:** Leftover Chicken and Rice Casserole

**Dinner:** Spaghetti with Meat Sauce
- 1 pound pasta, 1 jar marinara, 1 pound ground beef, 1 onion

**Snack:** Peanut Butter Energy Balls
- 1 cup peanut butter, 1/2 cup honey, 1 cup oats

### Wednesday

**Breakfast:** Overnight Oats (leftover)

**Lunch:** Leftover Spaghetti

**Dinner:** Rice and Beans
- 2 cups rice, 2 cans black beans, 1 onion, spices

**Snack:** Granola Bars (leftover)

### Thursday

**Breakfast:** Pancakes (leftover, reheated)

**Lunch:** Leftover Rice and Beans

**Dinner:** Tuna Pasta Salad
- 1 pound pasta, 2 cans tuna, 1/2 cup mayonnaise, 1 can vegetables

**Snack:** Peanut Butter Energy Balls (leftover)

### Friday

**Breakfast:** Overnight Oats (new batch)

**Lunch:** Leftover Tuna Pasta Salad

**Dinner:** Pantry Chili
- 2 pounds ground beef, 2 cans kidney beans, 1 can tomatoes, 1 can paste, spices

**Snack:** Granola Bars (new batch)

### Saturday

**Breakfast:** Pancakes (new batch)

**Lunch:** Leftover Chili

**Dinner:** Coconut Curry with Rice
- 2 cups rice, 1 can coconut milk, 1 can chickpeas, 1 onion, curry powder

**Snack:** Peanut Butter Energy Balls (new batch)

### Sunday

**Breakfast:** Overnight Oats (leftover)

**Lunch:** Leftover Coconut Curry

**Dinner:** Pantry Minestrone Soup
- 1 can tomatoes, 2 cans beans, 1 cup pasta, 4 cups broth, 1 onion

**Snack:** Granola Bars (leftover)

## Meal Prep Schedule

**Sunday (2 hours):**
- Cook rice (make extra for the week)
- Cook pasta (make extra for the week)
- Make granola bars
- Prep vegetables
- Make overnight oats for Monday

**Wednesday (30 minutes):**
- Make new batch of overnight oats
- Make new batch of granola bars
- Prep any remaining ingredients

## Tips for Meal Planning Success

1. **Shop on Sunday**: One trip, set for the week
2. **Prep in batches**: Cook rice, pasta, beans in large batches
3. **Use leftovers**: Plan meals that use leftovers
4. **Keep it simple**: Don't overcomplicate
5. **Be flexible**: Swap meals if needed
