# Recipes with Pantry Staples: 20 Meals You Can Make Right Now

Raiding your pantry doesn't have to mean boring meals. With a few basic staples, you can create delicious, satisfying dinners without a trip to the store. Here's how to turn those canned goods, pasta, and grains into restaurant-quality meals.

## Essential Pantry Staples Every Kitchen Should Have

Before we dive into recipes, here's what makes a well-stocked pantry:

**Grains & Pasta:**
- Rice (white, brown, or jasmine)
- Pasta (spaghetti, penne, or your favorite)
- Oats or quinoa

**Proteins:**
- Canned beans (black, chickpeas, kidney beans)
- Canned tuna or salmon
- Canned chicken
- Peanut butter or other nut butters

**Canned Goods:**
- Diced tomatoes
- Tomato paste
- Coconut milk
- Broth (chicken, vegetable, or beef)

**Spices & Seasonings:**
- Salt, pepper, garlic powder
- Italian seasoning
- Chili powder or cumin
- Soy sauce or Worcestershire sauce

**Oils & Vinegars:**
- Olive oil
- Vegetable oil
- Balsamic or apple cider vinegar

## Quick Pantry Recipes (30 Minutes or Less)

### 1. Pantry Pasta with Canned Tomatoes

**Cook time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound pasta
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Parmesan cheese (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Cook pasta according to package directions
2. While pasta cooks, heat olive oil in a large pan
3. Add garlic, cook 1 minute
4. Add tomatoes and seasoning, simmer 10 minutes
5. Toss with pasta, season with salt and pepper
6. Top with Parmesan if available

### 2. Rice and Beans

**Cook time:** 25 minutes | **Serves:** 4

A complete protein that's filling and flavorful.

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups cooked rice
- 2 cans (15 oz) black beans, drained
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon chili powder
- Salt to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Sauté onion and garlic until soft
2. Add beans, cumin, and chili powder
3. Simmer 10 minutes, season with salt
4. Serve over rice

### 3. Tuna Pasta Salad

**Cook time:** 15 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound pasta
- 2 cans (5 oz) tuna, drained
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise or olive oil
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice or vinegar
- Salt and pepper to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Cook pasta, drain and cool
2. Mix tuna with mayonnaise and lemon juice
3. Toss with pasta
4. Season with salt and pepper
5. Chill before serving

## Hearty Pantry Meals

### 4. Pantry Chili

**Cook time:** 30 minutes | **Serves:** 6

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cans (15 oz) kidney beans
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 can (6 oz) tomato paste
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons chili powder
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- Salt to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Sauté onion and garlic
2. Add spices, cook 1 minute
3. Add tomatoes, paste, and beans
4. Simmer 20 minutes
5. Season with salt

### 5. Coconut Curry with Rice

**Cook time:** 25 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups cooked rice
- 1 can (14 oz) coconut milk
- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 tablespoons curry powder
- 1 teaspoon turmeric
- Salt to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Sauté onion until soft
2. Add curry powder and turmeric, cook 1 minute
3. Add coconut milk and chickpeas
4. Simmer 15 minutes
5. Serve over rice

### 6. Pantry Minestrone Soup

**Cook time:** 35 minutes | **Serves:** 6

**Ingredients:**
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 can (15 oz) kidney beans
- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas
- 1 cup pasta
- 4 cups vegetable broth
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- Salt and pepper to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Sauté onion and garlic
2. Add tomatoes, beans, and broth
3. Bring to boil, add pasta
4. Simmer 15 minutes until pasta is tender
5. Season with salt and pepper

## Budget-Friendly Options

### 7. Peanut Butter Noodles

**Cook time:** 15 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound pasta
- 1/4 cup peanut butter
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon vinegar
- 1 tablespoon honey or sugar
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- Red pepper flakes (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Cook pasta according to package
2. Mix peanut butter, soy sauce, vinegar, and honey
3. Add garlic and red pepper flakes
4. Toss with pasta
5. Add pasta water if needed to thin sauce

### 8. Pantry Fried Rice

**Cook time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 4

Use any leftover rice and vegetables you have.

**Ingredients:**
- 3 cups cooked rice
- 2 eggs
- 1 can (15 oz) mixed vegetables, drained
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon oil
- 2 green onions (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Scramble eggs in hot pan, remove
2. Add vegetables, cook until warm
3. Add rice, break up clumps
4. Return eggs, add soy sauce
5. Garnish with green onions

### 9. Canned Chicken Salad

**Cook time:** 10 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cans (12.5 oz) chicken, drained
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1 tablespoon mustard
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- Salt and pepper to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Shred chicken with fork
2. Mix with mayonnaise, mustard, and lemon juice
3. Season with salt and pepper
4. Serve on bread or crackers

## International Pantry Dishes

### 10. Pantry Pasta Carbonara

**Cook time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound pasta
- 4 eggs
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
- 4 slices bacon or pancetta (if available)
- Black pepper to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Cook pasta, reserve 1 cup pasta water
2. Cook bacon until crispy
3. Mix eggs and Parmesan
4. Toss hot pasta with bacon
5. Add egg mixture, toss quickly
6. Add pasta water to create sauce
7. Season with black pepper

### 11. Pantry Risotto

**Cook time:** 30 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 1/2 cups arborio rice
- 4 cups warm broth
- 1 onion, diced
- 1/2 cup white wine (optional)
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
- 2 tablespoons butter

**Instructions:**
1. Sauté onion until soft
2. Add rice, toast 2 minutes
3. Add wine (if using), cook until absorbed
4. Add broth 1 cup at a time, stirring constantly
5. Cook until rice is creamy, 20-25 minutes
6. Stir in butter and Parmesan

## Meal Prep Pantry Recipes

### 12. Overnight Oats (5 Ways)

**Prep time:** 5 minutes | **Serves:** 1

Perfect for breakfast meal prep.

**Base Recipe:**
- 1/2 cup oats
- 1/2 cup milk or water
- Sweetener (honey, maple syrup, or sugar)
- Toppings (nuts, fruit, spices)

**Variations:**
- Peanut butter and banana
- Apple cinnamon
- Chocolate and coconut
- Berry vanilla
- Maple and nuts

### 13. Pantry Granola Bars

**Cook time:** 25 minutes | **Makes:** 12 bars

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups oats
- 1/2 cup peanut butter
- 1/3 cup honey
- 1/2 cup nuts (optional)
- 1/4 cup dried fruit (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Mix all ingredients
2. Press into baking dish
3. Bake 350°F for 20 minutes
4. Cool completely before cutting

## Tips for Pantry Cooking

1. **Stock rotation**: Use oldest items first
2. **Herbs and spices**: Small amounts add big flavor
3. **Canned tomatoes**: Better than fresh for sauces
4. **Bean liquid**: Aquafaba from chickpeas can replace eggs
5. **Pasta water**: Starchy water helps bind sauces

**Nice-to-Haves:**
- Quinoa or other grains
- Canned coconut milk
- Peanut butter
- Oils and vinegars
- Nuts and seeds
- Dried fruits
