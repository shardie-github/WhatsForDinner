# Pantry-Only Recipes for Busy Weeknights: 12 Quick & Easy Meals

Busy weeknights call for simple solutions. When you're short on time and energy, these pantry-only recipes are your secret weapon. No grocery shopping required—just open your pantry and get cooking.

## Why Pantry-Only Recipes Work

Pantry-only recipes are perfect for busy weeknights because they:
- **Save time**: No grocery shopping needed
- **Reduce stress**: Use what you already have
- **Save money**: No impulse purchases
- **Are flexible**: Easy to adapt to what you have
- **Cook fast**: Most recipes are 30 minutes or less

## Quick Pantry Dinners (20-30 Minutes)

### 1. One-Pan Pasta with Canned Tomatoes

**Cook time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound pasta
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- Salt and pepper

**Instructions:**
1. Cook pasta according to package
2. While pasta cooks, heat oil and garlic
3. Add tomatoes and seasoning
4. Simmer 10 minutes
5. Toss with pasta

### 2. Quick Rice and Beans

**Cook time:** 25 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups cooked rice
- 2 cans black beans
- 1 onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- Salt to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Sauté onion and garlic
2. Add beans and cumin
3. Simmer 10 minutes
4. Serve over rice

### 3. Tuna Pasta Salad

**Cook time:** 15 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound pasta
- 2 cans tuna
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- Salt and pepper

**Instructions:**
1. Cook and cool pasta
2. Mix tuna with mayonnaise and lemon
3. Toss with pasta
4. Season to taste

## Hearty Pantry Meals

### 4. Pantry Chili

**Cook time:** 30 minutes | **Serves:** 6

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cans kidney beans
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 1 can tomato paste
- 1 onion
- 2 tablespoons chili powder
- 1 teaspoon cumin

**Instructions:**
1. Sauté onion
2. Add spices
3. Add tomatoes, paste, and beans
4. Simmer 20 minutes

### 5. Coconut Curry with Rice

**Cook time:** 25 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups cooked rice
- 1 can coconut milk
- 1 can chickpeas
- 1 onion
- 2 tablespoons curry powder

**Instructions:**
1. Sauté onion
2. Add curry powder
3. Add coconut milk and chickpeas
4. Simmer 15 minutes
5. Serve over rice

### 6. Pantry Minestrone

**Cook time:** 35 minutes | **Serves:** 6

**Ingredients:**
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 2 cans beans (kidney, chickpeas)
- 1 cup pasta
- 4 cups broth
- 1 onion
- Italian seasoning

**Instructions:**
1. Sauté onion
2. Add tomatoes, beans, broth
3. Bring to boil
4. Add pasta
5. Simmer 15 minutes

## Budget-Friendly Options

### 7. Peanut Butter Noodles

**Cook time:** 15 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound pasta
- 1/4 cup peanut butter
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon vinegar
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 2 cloves garlic

**Instructions:**
1. Cook pasta
2. Mix peanut butter, soy sauce, vinegar, honey
3. Add garlic
4. Toss with pasta

### 8. Pantry Fried Rice

**Cook time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 3 cups cooked rice
- 2 eggs
- 1 can mixed vegetables
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon oil

**Instructions:**
1. Scramble eggs, remove
2. Add vegetables
3. Add rice
4. Return eggs
5. Add soy sauce

### 9. Canned Chicken Salad

**Cook time:** 10 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cans chicken
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1 tablespoon mustard
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- Salt and pepper

**Instructions:**
1. Shred chicken
2. Mix with mayonnaise, mustard, lemon
3. Season to taste
4. Serve on bread

## Meal Prep Favorites

### 10. Overnight Oats

**Prep time:** 5 minutes | **Serves:** 1

**Ingredients:**
- 1/2 cup oats
- 1/2 cup milk
- 2 tablespoons peanut butter
- 1 tablespoon honey
- Pinch of salt

**Instructions:**
1. Mix all ingredients
2. Refrigerate overnight
3. Enjoy in the morning

### 11. Pantry Granola Bars

**Cook time:** 25 minutes | **Makes:** 12 bars

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups oats
- 1/2 cup peanut butter
- 1/3 cup honey
- 1/2 cup nuts (optional)
- 1/4 cup dried fruit (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Mix all ingredients
2. Press into baking dish
3. Bake 350°F for 20 minutes
4. Cool before cutting

### 12. Pantry Pasta Carbonara

**Cook time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound pasta
- 4 eggs
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
- 4 slices bacon
- Black pepper

**Instructions:**
1. Cook pasta, reserve pasta water
2. Cook bacon until crispy
3. Mix eggs and Parmesan
4. Toss hot pasta with bacon
5. Add egg mixture
6. Add pasta water to create sauce

## Time-Saving Tips

1. **Prep ahead**: Keep pantry staples organized
2. **Use shortcuts**: Canned goods, pre-made sauces
3. **Batch cook**: Make extra rice or pasta
4. **One-pan meals**: Less cleanup
5. **Keep it simple**: Fewer ingredients = faster cooking
