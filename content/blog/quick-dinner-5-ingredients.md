# Quick Dinner Ideas with 5 Ingredients: 15 Fast & Easy Recipes

Busy weeknights don't have to mean takeout or frozen dinners. With just 5 ingredients (plus salt, pepper, and oil), you can create delicious, satisfying meals in 30 minutes or less. Here are 15 recipes that prove simple can be spectacular.

## The 5-Ingredient Philosophy

The key to successful 5-ingredient cooking is choosing ingredients that pack a punch:
- **Flavorful proteins**: Chicken, salmon, ground beef
- **Aromatic vegetables**: Garlic, onions, bell peppers
- **Sauces and seasonings**: Soy sauce, Italian dressing, pesto
- **Grains or pasta**: Rice, pasta, quinoa
- **Finishing touches**: Cheese, herbs, lemon

## Super Fast Recipes (15-20 Minutes)

### 1. Lemon Garlic Chicken

**Cook time:** 15 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 4 chicken breasts
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 lemons, juiced
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Fresh parsley (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Season chicken with salt and pepper
2. Heat oil in large skillet
3. Cook chicken 6-7 minutes per side
4. Add garlic, cook 1 minute
5. Add lemon juice, simmer 2 minutes
6. Garnish with parsley

### 2. 5-Ingredient Pasta

**Cook time:** 15 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound pasta
- 1 jar (24 oz) marinara sauce
- 1 pound ground beef
- 1 onion, diced
- Parmesan cheese

**Instructions:**
1. Cook pasta according to package
2. Brown beef with onion
3. Add marinara sauce, simmer 5 minutes
4. Toss with pasta
5. Top with Parmesan

### 3. Salmon with Soy Glaze

**Cook time:** 15 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 4 salmon fillets
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons honey
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon oil

**Instructions:**
1. Mix soy sauce, honey, and garlic
2. Heat oil in skillet
3. Cook salmon 4-5 minutes per side
4. Pour glaze over salmon
5. Simmer 2 minutes until glazed

## One-Pan Wonders (20-25 Minutes)

### 4. Sheet Pan Chicken and Vegetables

**Cook time:** 25 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 4 chicken thighs
- 1 pound baby potatoes
- 2 bell peppers, sliced
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Italian seasoning

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 425°F
2. Toss everything with oil and seasoning
3. Arrange on sheet pan
4. Bake 25 minutes until chicken is cooked

### 5. 5-Ingredient Stir Fry

**Cook time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound chicken, sliced
- 1 bag frozen vegetables
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons sesame oil
- 2 cups cooked rice

**Instructions:**
1. Heat sesame oil in wok
2. Cook chicken until done
3. Add vegetables, cook until tender
4. Add soy sauce, toss
5. Serve over rice

### 6. Sausage and Peppers

**Cook time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound Italian sausage
- 2 bell peppers, sliced
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 2 tablespoons olive oil

**Instructions:**
1. Brown sausage, remove
2. Sauté peppers and onion
3. Add tomatoes and sausage
4. Simmer 10 minutes
5. Serve hot

## Quick Comfort Food (25-30 Minutes)

### 7. 5-Ingredient Mac and Cheese

**Cook time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound pasta
- 2 cups shredded cheddar
- 2 cups milk
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 tablespoons flour

**Instructions:**
1. Cook pasta according to package
2. Melt butter, whisk in flour
3. Add milk, whisk until smooth
4. Add cheese, stir until melted
5. Toss with pasta

### 8. Simple Beef Tacos

**Cook time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound ground beef
- 1 packet taco seasoning
- 8 taco shells
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- Sour cream (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Brown beef, drain fat
2. Add taco seasoning and water
3. Simmer 5 minutes
4. Fill taco shells
5. Top with cheese and sour cream

### 9. Chicken and Rice Casserole

**Cook time:** 30 minutes | **Serves:** 6

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups cooked rice
- 2 cups cooked, shredded chicken
- 1 can cream of chicken soup
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- 1/2 cup breadcrumbs

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 375°F
2. Mix rice, chicken, and soup
3. Transfer to baking dish
4. Top with cheese and breadcrumbs
5. Bake 25 minutes

## Healthy 5-Ingredient Meals

### 10. Greek Chicken Salad

**Cook time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 4 chicken breasts
- 1 head romaine lettuce
- 1 cup feta cheese
- 1/2 cup Greek dressing
- 1/2 cup Kalamata olives

**Instructions:**
1. Season and cook chicken
2. Slice chicken
3. Toss lettuce with dressing
4. Top with chicken, feta, and olives

### 11. Quinoa Bowl

**Cook time:** 25 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups cooked quinoa
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 1 cup salsa
- 1/2 cup shredded cheese

**Instructions:**
1. Divide quinoa among bowls
2. Top with beans
3. Add avocado and salsa
4. Top with cheese

### 12. Baked Salmon and Asparagus

**Cook time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 4 salmon fillets
- 1 pound asparagus
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 lemons
- Fresh dill (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 400°F
2. Arrange salmon and asparagus on sheet pan
3. Drizzle with oil and lemon juice
4. Season with salt and pepper
5. Bake 15-18 minutes
6. Garnish with dill

## International Flavors

### 13. 5-Ingredient Pad Thai

**Cook time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound rice noodles
- 1/2 cup peanut butter
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 eggs
- 2 cups bean sprouts

**Instructions:**
1. Cook noodles according to package
2. Scramble eggs, remove
3. Mix peanut butter and soy sauce
4. Toss noodles with sauce
5. Add eggs and bean sprouts

### 14. Simple Margherita Pasta

**Cook time:** 15 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound pasta
- 2 cups cherry tomatoes
- 1 cup fresh mozzarella
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- Fresh basil

**Instructions:**
1. Cook pasta according to package
2. Halve tomatoes
3. Toss pasta with oil
4. Add tomatoes and mozzarella
5. Garnish with basil

### 15. 5-Ingredient Curry

**Cook time:** 25 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound chicken, cubed
- 1 can (14 oz) coconut milk
- 2 tablespoons curry powder
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cups cooked rice

**Instructions:**
1. Brown chicken, remove
2. Sauté onion
3. Add curry powder, cook 1 minute
4. Add coconut milk and chicken
5. Simmer 15 minutes
6. Serve over rice

## Tips for 5-Ingredient Success

1. **Quality matters**: Use the best ingredients you can afford
2. **Season well**: Salt, pepper, and oil are your friends
3. **Prep ahead**: Chop vegetables the night before
4. **Keep it simple**: Don't overthink - simple is better
5. **Use what you have**: Substitutions are always okay

## Time-Saving Hacks

- **Pre-cut vegetables**: Save chopping time
- **Pre-cooked proteins**: Rotisserie chicken, cooked shrimp
- **Jarred sauces**: Marinara, pesto, curry paste
- **Frozen vegetables**: Already chopped and ready
- **Microwave rice**: Perfect for quick meals
