# Keto Recipes with Pantry Staples: 10 Low-Carb Meals You Can Make Right Now

Following a keto diet doesn't have to mean expensive specialty ingredients. With the right pantry staples, you can create delicious, low-carb meals that keep you in ketosis. Here are 10 keto recipes using ingredients you probably already have.

## Keto Pantry Staples

Before we dive into recipes, here's what you should have for keto cooking:

**Proteins:**
- Canned tuna or salmon
- Canned chicken
- Canned sardines
- Beef jerky (low sugar)

**Fats:**
- Coconut oil
- Olive oil
- Avocado oil
- Coconut milk (full-fat)
- Nuts and seeds (almonds, walnuts, chia seeds)

**Vegetables (Canned):**
- Canned mushrooms
- Canned artichoke hearts
- Canned olives
- Pickles (low sugar)

**Condiments:**
- Mustard (no sugar)
- Soy sauce or coconut aminos
- Hot sauce
- Apple cider vinegar

**Spices:**
- Salt, pepper, garlic powder
- Italian seasoning
- Chili powder, cumin
- Paprika

## Quick Keto Recipes

### 1. Keto Tuna Salad

**Cook time:** 5 minutes | **Serves:** 2

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cans (5 oz) tuna, drained
- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 1 tablespoon mustard
- 1/4 cup pickles, chopped
- 1/4 cup celery (if available)
- Salt and pepper

**Instructions:**
1. Drain tuna well
2. Mix with mayonnaise and mustard
3. Add pickles and celery
4. Season with salt and pepper
5. Serve on lettuce wraps or eat with a fork

**Net Carbs:** 2g per serving

### 2. Keto Coconut Curry

**Cook time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cans (14 oz) coconut milk
- 1 can (15 oz) mushrooms, drained
- 1 can (14 oz) artichoke hearts, drained
- 2 tablespoons curry powder
- 1 teaspoon turmeric
- 2 cloves garlic (or garlic powder)
- Salt to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Heat coconut milk in a pan
2. Add curry powder and turmeric
3. Add mushrooms and artichoke hearts
4. Simmer 15 minutes
5. Season with salt

**Net Carbs:** 5g per serving

### 3. Keto Peanut Butter Smoothie

**Cook time:** 5 minutes | **Serves:** 1

**Ingredients:**
- 1/2 cup coconut milk
- 2 tablespoons peanut butter (no sugar added)
- 1 tablespoon cocoa powder (unsweetened)
- 1 tablespoon chia seeds
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Ice (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Blend all ingredients
2. Add ice if desired
3. Enjoy immediately

**Net Carbs:** 4g per serving

## Hearty Keto Meals

### 4. Keto Chili (No Beans)

**Cook time:** 30 minutes | **Serves:** 6

**Ingredients:**
- 2 pounds ground beef
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 can (6 oz) tomato paste
- 2 tablespoons chili powder
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- Salt to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Brown ground beef
2. Add spices, cook 1 minute
3. Add tomatoes and paste
4. Simmer 20 minutes
5. Season with salt

**Net Carbs:** 6g per serving

### 5. Keto Canned Chicken Salad

**Cook time:** 10 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cans (12.5 oz) chicken, drained
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1/4 cup pickles, chopped
- 1 tablespoon mustard
- 1/4 cup celery (if available)
- Salt and pepper

**Instructions:**
1. Shred chicken
2. Mix with mayonnaise, mustard, pickles
3. Add celery if available
4. Season with salt and pepper
5. Serve on lettuce wraps

**Net Carbs:** 3g per serving

### 6. Keto Artichoke and Olive Salad

**Cook time:** 10 minutes | **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cans (14 oz) artichoke hearts, drained
- 1 can (6 oz) black olives, drained
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice (or vinegar)
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- Salt and pepper

**Instructions:**
1. Chop artichoke hearts
2. Mix with olives
3. Whisk olive oil, lemon juice, and seasoning
4. Toss with artichokes and olives
5. Season with salt and pepper

**Net Carbs:** 4g per serving

## Keto Snacks and Sides

### 7. Keto Nuts and Seeds Mix

**Prep time:** 5 minutes | **Serves:** 8

**Ingredients:**
- 1 cup almonds
- 1 cup walnuts
- 1/2 cup pumpkin seeds
- 1/2 cup chia seeds
- 2 tablespoons coconut oil
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder

**Instructions:**
1. Mix all nuts and seeds
2. Toss with melted coconut oil
3. Season with salt and garlic powder
4. Roast at 350°F for 10 minutes (optional)
5. Store in airtight container

**Net Carbs:** 3g per serving

### 8. Keto Fat Bombs

**Prep time:** 10 minutes | **Makes:** 12

**Ingredients:**
- 1/2 cup coconut oil
- 1/4 cup peanut butter (no sugar)
- 2 tablespoons cocoa powder
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt

**Instructions:**
1. Melt coconut oil
2. Mix with peanut butter
3. Add cocoa powder and vanilla
4. Pour into molds or ice cube tray
5. Freeze 1 hour

**Net Carbs:** 1g per serving

### 9. Keto Sardine Salad

**Cook time:** 5 minutes | **Serves:** 2

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cans (4 oz) sardines
- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 1 tablespoon mustard
- 1/4 cup pickles, chopped
- Salt and pepper

**Instructions:**
1. Drain sardines
2. Mix with mayonnaise and mustard
3. Add pickles
4. Season with salt and pepper

**Net Carbs:** 2g per serving

### 10. Keto Coconut Chia Pudding

**Prep time:** 5 minutes (+ overnight) | **Serves:** 2

**Ingredients:**
- 1 can (14 oz) coconut milk
- 1/4 cup chia seeds
- 1 tablespoon cocoa powder (optional)
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Sweetener (optional, stevia or erythritol)

**Instructions:**
1. Mix all ingredients
2. Refrigerate overnight
3. Stir before serving
4. Top with nuts if desired

**Net Carbs:** 4g per serving

## Tips for Keto Pantry Cooking

1. **Check labels**: Avoid added sugars in canned goods
2. **Focus on fats**: Use full-fat coconut milk, oils
3. **Watch carbs**: Canned vegetables can have hidden carbs
4. **Stock up**: Keep keto-friendly pantry items on hand
5. **Be creative**: Combine ingredients in new ways

## Common Keto Mistakes with Pantry Items

1. **Canned fruits**: Too high in carbs (avoid)
2. **Canned beans**: Not keto-friendly (avoid)
3. **Regular pasta**: High in carbs (avoid)
4. **Sugary condiments**: Check labels carefully
5. **Low-fat items**: Often have added sugars
