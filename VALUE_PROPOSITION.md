# Why What's for Dinner Exists

## The Daily Struggle

Every single day, at roughly 6 PM, millions of people around the world face the same moment of decision paralysis. They open their refrigerator, stare at the contents, and ask themselves: "What am I going to make for dinner?"

This isn't a trivial question. It's a moment that combines:
- **Exhaustion** from a long day
- **Decision fatigue** from making choices all day
- **Time pressure** to get food on the table
- **Guilt** about food waste or ordering takeout again
- **Frustration** with meal planning apps that don't understand their actual kitchen

We built What's for Dinner to solve this exact moment.

---

## The Pain Points We Solve

### 1. The Pantry Blindness Problem

**The Problem**: You have food in your pantry, but you don't remember what's there. You buy duplicates. Things expire. You end up ordering takeout even though you have ingredients at home.

**Our Solution**: Smart pantry tracking that learns what you keep in stock. Get alerts when things are expiring. Discover recipes you can make right now with what you actually have.

**The Value**: Save money, reduce waste, and never wonder "what can I make?" again.

### 2. The Recipe Overload Problem

**The Problem**: Recipe apps show you thousands of options, but most require ingredients you don't have. You spend 20 minutes scrolling, get frustrated, and order pizza.

**Our Solution**: AI-powered meal suggestions that only show recipes you can actually make right now, based on your pantry, preferences, and available time.

**The Value**: Get from "I'm hungry" to "I'm cooking" in under 2 minutes.

### 3. The Meal Planning Burden

**The Problem**: Meal planning feels like work. It takes hours. You plan meals you never make. You forget ingredients at the store. The whole system breaks down by Wednesday.

**Our Solution**: Automated meal planning that generates a week's worth of meals in seconds. Automatically builds shopping lists. Adapts to your schedule and preferences.

**The Value**: Meal planning becomes effortless, not exhausting.

### 4. The Multi-Platform Fragmentation

**The Problem**: Your meal plan is on your phone, your shopping list is on a sticky note, your recipes are in a cookbook. Nothing syncs. Nothing works together.

**Our Solution**: One app that works seamlessly across iOS, Android, and Web. Everything syncs automatically. Start planning on your phone, finish cooking with your tablet.

**The Value**: One system, everywhere, always in sync.

## Why This Matters

### Food Waste is a Crisis

The average American household wastes $1,500 worth of food per year. Much of this waste happens because people forget what they have, buy duplicates, or let things expire.

**What's for Dinner reduces waste** by:
- Tracking what you have
- Alerting you before things expire
- Suggesting recipes that use expiring ingredients
- Helping you use what you buy

### Mental Load is Real

Meal planning is cognitive work. It requires:
- Remembering what you have
- Deciding what to make
- Planning shopping trips
- Managing dietary preferences
- Balancing nutrition goals

**What's for Dinner reduces mental load** by automating the thinking, so you can focus on the cooking.

### Time is Precious

People spend hours each week on meal planning, shopping, and deciding what to cook. That's time that could be spent with family, on hobbies, or just relaxing.

**What's for Dinner saves time** by:
- Instant meal suggestions
- Automated shopping lists
- One-tap recipe access
- Seamless cross-platform sync
