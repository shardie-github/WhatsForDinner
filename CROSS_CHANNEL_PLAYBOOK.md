# Cross-Channel Marketing Playbook: Capturing Indirect Demand

**Purpose**: Detailed playbook for capturing customer demand through parenting, church, wellness, and other indirect channels.

---

## Channel 1: Parenting Organizations

### Target Audience
- **Primary**: Parents aged 28-45 with children ages 2-12
- **Pain Points**: Picky eaters, time constraints, budget concerns
- **Channels**: Parenting blogs, Facebook groups, Instagram, podcasts

### Messaging

**Primary Message:**
> "Stop fighting with picky eaters. Get kid-approved recipes from ingredients you already have—in 30 seconds."

**Pain Points:**
- "My kids won't eat what I cook"
- "I spend 20 minutes deciding what to make"
- "I waste money on groceries kids don't eat"

**Value Propositions:**
- Kid-friendly recipe filter
- Family meal planning
- Budget-friendly recipes
- Time-saving (15+ minutes per meal)

### Tactics

#### 1. Content Marketing
- [ ] Create 10 parenting-focused blog posts:
  - "How to Meal Plan with Picky Eaters"
  - "Kid-Approved Recipes from Pantry Staples"
  - "Family Meal Planning on a Budget"
  - "Quick Weeknight Meals for Busy Parents"
  - "Meal Prep for School Lunches"
  - "Healthy Snacks Kids Will Actually Eat"
  - "One-Pot Meals for Families"
  - "Meal Planning for Picky Toddlers"
  - "Budget-Friendly Family Dinners"
  - "Meal Planning Reduces Family Stress"

#### 2. Partnership Targets
- [ ] **Parenting Blogs** (target 20):
  - Scary Mommy
  - What to Expect
  - Mom.me
  - The Spruce Eats (family section)
  - Verywell Family
  - **Outreach Template**: "Hi [Name], I noticed your article on [topic]. We help parents solve meal planning with AI-powered recipes. Would you be interested in a guest post or partnership?"

- [ ] **Parenting Facebook Groups** (target 10):
  - "Mom Meal Planning Ideas" (50K+ members)
  - "Budget-Friendly Family Meals" (30K+ members)
  - "Working Mom Meal Prep" (25K+ members)
  - **Approach**: Provide value first (free recipes), then introduce product

- [ ] **Parenting Podcasts** (target 5):
  - "The Mom Hour"
  - "Don't Mom Alone"
  - "Coffee + Crumbs"
  - **Pitch**: "We help thousands of parents save 15+ minutes per meal with AI-powered recipe suggestions. Would you like a demo?"

#### 3. Influencer Partnerships
- [ ] **Micro-Influencers** (10K-100K followers, target 10):
  - Mom food bloggers
  - Meal prep Instagram accounts
  - Family lifestyle influencers
  - **Offer**: Free premium account + affiliate program (20% commission)

#### 4. Landing Page
- [ ] Create `/for-families` landing page
- [ ] Highlight:
  - Kid-friendly recipes
  - Family meal planning
  - Time-saving benefits
  - Budget-friendly options
  - Testimonials from parents

#### 5. Product Features
- [ ] Add "Kid-Friendly" recipe filter
- [ ] Create family meal planning feature (4+ servings)
- [ ] Add "Picky Eater" mode (hide vegetables, familiar flavors)

### Success Metrics
- **Target**: 500 signups from parenting channels in 90 days
- **Conversion Rate**: 3-5% (visitors → signups)
- **CAC**: <$20
- **LTV**: $120+ (10-month retention)

- **Pain Points**: Low wellness program engagement, need differentiated benefits
- **Channels**: B2B sales, HR conferences, wellness platforms

### Messaging

**Primary Message:**
> "Increase employee wellness engagement. Meal planning improves nutrition, reduces stress, and saves time."

**Pain Points:**
- "Wellness programs have low engagement"
- "Employees want practical benefits"
- "Nutrition is hard to address"

**Value Propositions:**
- White-label solution
- Employee engagement analytics
- HIPAA-compliant
- Proven ROI (reduced stress, improved health)

### Tactics

#### 1. B2B Sales Process
- [ ] Create B2B sales deck (20 slides)
- [ ] Identify 50 target companies:
  - Tech companies (high stress)
  - Healthcare (employee wellness focus)
  - Finance (work-life balance)
  - **CRM Setup**: Track outreach, demos, proposals

- [ ] Outreach Template:
  > "Hi [Name], I noticed [Company] has a wellness program. We help companies increase engagement by offering meal planning as a benefit. Employees save 15+ minutes per meal and reduce stress. Would you like a 15-minute demo?"

#### 2. Pilot Program
- [ ] Offer 3-month pilot (free)
- [ ] Include:
  - Up to 1,000 employees
  - Analytics dashboard
  - Monthly check-ins
  - Success metrics tracking

#### 3. Enterprise Features
- [ ] Single Sign-On (SSO)
- [ ] Admin dashboard (usage analytics)
- [ ] Custom branding
- [ ] Employee engagement reports
- [ ] HIPAA compliance documentation

#### 4. Pricing
- [ ] $5-10 per employee/month
- [ ] Volume discounts (20% for 1,000+ employees)
- [ ] Annual plans (2 months free)

#### 5. Content Marketing
- [ ] Create 3 B2B-focused blog posts:
  - "Employee Meal Planning Benefits"
  - "Corporate Wellness: Nutrition Programs"
  - "Meal Planning for Remote Workers"

### Success Metrics
- **Target**: 3 corporate customers, $10K+ ARR in 90 days
- **Conversion Rate**: 5-10% (demos → customers)
