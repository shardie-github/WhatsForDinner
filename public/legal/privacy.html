Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

1. Introduction

What's for Dinner? ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information (email, password)
  • Pantry inventory data
  • Recipe preferences and meal plans
  • Payment information (processed securely via Stripe)

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information (OS, device model, unique identifiers)
  • Usage analytics (app interactions, features used)
  • Crash reports and error logs
  • IP address and location (general area only)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and improve our services
  • Personalize meal suggestions based on your pantry
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions
  • Send push notifications about meal ideas
  • Analyze usage patterns to enhance app experience
  • Ensure app security and prevent fraud

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Service Providers: Supabase (database/auth), Stripe (payments), PostHog (analytics), Sentry (crash reporting)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect rights
  • Business Transfers: In connection with mergers or acquisitions

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption, secure storage, and access controls. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data (via Settings ? Account ? Delete Account)
  • Opt-out: Disable analytics tracking in Settings
  • Export: Export your data in a machine-readable format

7. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed and stored in servers outside your country. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via in-app notification or email.

10. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@whatsfordinner.app
  • Support: App Support

11. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to know what personal information is collected and the right to opt-out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information).

12. GDPR (European Users)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have additional rights under GDPR, including the right to object to processing and the right to data portability. Our legal basis for processing is your consent and our legitimate interest in providing services.
