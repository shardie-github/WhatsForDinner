## Product Hunt Description Template

### Title
**What's for Dinner? - Pantry-First AI Meal Planner**

### Tagline
**Never stare at your pantry confused again. Get personalized recipes from ingredients you already have in under 30 seconds.**

### Description

**The Problem:**
45% of meal planning app users say "Can't find recipes with ingredients I have." Most apps make you browse recipes, create shopping lists, and plan ahead. But what about when you're hungry *right now* and want to use what's already in your pantry?

**The Solution:**
What's for Dinner? is the **only pantry-first meal planner**. Start with what you have, not what you need. Enter your ingredients (or use voice: "What can I make with chicken and rice?") and get personalized recipe suggestions in under 30 seconds.

**Why We're Different:**
- ✅ **Pantry-First Approach**: Unique in the market—no competitor offers this
- ✅ **Fastest Time-to-Value**:** <30 seconds (competitors: 3-10+ minutes)
- ✅ **Advanced AI Personalization**: AI learns your preferences over time
- ✅ **Voice Interface** (Coming Soon): "What can I make with X?"

**Key Features:**
- Pantry-first recipe generation
- AI-powered personalization
- 30-second time-to-value
- Mobile app (iOS & Android)
- Grocery integration (Coming Soon)
- Voice interface (Coming Soon)

**Try It Free:**
[whatsfordinner.com](https://whatsfordinner.com)

**What Makes Us Different:**
- vs. Yummly: They're recipe-first, we're pantry-first
- vs. AllRecipes: They're a recipe database, we generate personalized recipes
- vs. Mealime: They require planning ahead, we're spontaneous
