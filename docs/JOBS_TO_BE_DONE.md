## WHAT IS JOBS TO BE DONE?

Jobs to Be Done (JTBD) is a framework that focuses on the **functional, emotional, and social jobs** users hire our product to do. Instead of thinking about features, we think about the progress users want to make in their lives.

**Format:** "When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [expected outcome]."

---

## PRIMARY JOBS

### Job 1: Eliminate Decision Fatigue
**When** I'm standing in front of my fridge at 6 PM with no idea what to make,  
**I want** instant meal suggestions based on what I already have,  
**So I can** stop wasting time deciding and start cooking.

**Functional Job:** Get meal ideas quickly  
**Emotional Job:** Feel less stressed, more confident  
**Social Job:** Appear organized and capable to family

**Success Criteria:**
- Meal suggestion in <30 seconds
- Uses 3+ pantry items
- Recipe is actually cookable

**Current Solution:** Stare at fridge, order takeout, or make same old thing

---

### Job 2: Reduce Food Waste
**When** I buy ingredients with good intentions but forget what I have,  
**I want** to track my pantry and get alerts when things are expiring,  
**So I can** use ingredients before they go bad and feel less guilty about waste.

**Functional Job:** Track inventory, get expiration alerts  
**Emotional Job:** Feel responsible, environmentally conscious  
**Social Job:** Demonstrate sustainability values

**Success Criteria:**
- Expiration alerts 3 days before expiry
- Suggestions prioritize expiring items
- Reduce waste by 30%+

**Current Solution:** Throw away expired food, feel guilty

---

### Job 3: Save Time on Meal Planning
**When** I want to plan meals for the week but don't have hours to research recipes,  
**I want** AI to generate a meal plan based on my pantry and preferences,  
**So I can** spend my weekend doing fun things instead of meal planning.

**Functional Job:** Generate meal plan automatically  
**Emotional Job:** Feel free, less burdened  
**Social Job:** Have more time for family/friends

**Success Criteria:**
- Weekly meal plan in <5 minutes
- Shopping list generated automatically
- Plan is actually followed

**Current Solution:** Spend 2+ hours planning, or don't plan at all

---

### Job 4: Discover New Recipes
**When** I'm bored with my usual recipes but don't know what to try,  
**I want** personalized recipe suggestions that match my skill level and preferences,  
**So I can** expand my cooking repertoire and impress myself/family.

**Functional Job:** Find new recipes easily  
**Emotional Job:** Feel creative, accomplished  
**Social Job:** Impress others with cooking skills

**Success Criteria:**
- Discover 5+ new recipes per month
- Recipes match skill level
- 70%+ of recipes are rated 4+ stars

**Current Solution:** Browse Pinterest for hours, save recipes never used

---

### Job 5: Optimize Grocery Shopping
**When** I go grocery shopping but forget items or buy too much,  
**I want** a smart shopping list that only includes what I need,  
**So I can** shop efficiently and avoid multiple trips.

**Functional Job:** Generate accurate shopping lists  
**Emotional Job:** Feel organized, efficient  
**Social Job:** Appear prepared and thoughtful

**Success Criteria:**
- Shopping list includes only missing ingredients
- Grouped by category (produce, dairy, etc.)
- Reduces grocery trips by 50%+

**Current Solution:** Make mental list, forget items, make multiple trips

---

## SECONDARY JOBS

### Job 6: Cook Healthier Meals
**When** I want to eat healthier but don't know what to cook,  
**I want** healthy recipe suggestions that fit my dietary restrictions,  
**So I can** improve my health without sacrificing taste.

**Functional Job:** Find healthy recipes  
**Emotional Job:** Feel healthy, accomplished  
**Social Job:** Demonstrate health-consciousness

**Success Criteria:**
- 80%+ of suggestions fit dietary restrictions
- Recipes are actually healthy (not just labeled)
- Users cook 3+ healthy meals per week

---

### Job 7: Meal Prep Efficiently
**When** I want to meal prep for the week but don't know how to plan it,  
**I want** a meal prep plan with recipes that work well for batch cooking,  
**So I can** save time during the week and eat home-cooked meals.

**Functional Job:** Plan meal prep sessions  
**Emotional Job:** Feel prepared, efficient  
**Social Job:** Demonstrate organization skills

**Success Criteria:**
- Meal prep plan includes 5+ meals
- Recipes are batch-cooking friendly
- Saves 5+ hours per week

---

### Job 8: Reduce Cooking Stress
**When** I'm stressed about cooking because I don't know what to make,  
**I want** clear, step-by-step recipe instructions with ingredient checklists,  
**So I can** cook confidently without second-guessing myself.

**Functional Job:** Follow recipes easily  
**Emotional Job:** Feel confident, relaxed  
**Social Job:** Appear capable in the kitchen

**Success Criteria:**
- Recipe instructions are clear and actionable
- Ingredient checklist prevents mistakes
- 80%+ of recipes completed successfully

## COMPETITIVE JOBS ANALYSIS

### How Competitors Solve These Jobs

**AllRecipes / Food Network:**
- Job: Discover new recipes
- Gap: Requires shopping for specific ingredients (doesn't use what you have)

**HelloFresh / Blue Apron:**
- Job: Save time on meal planning
- Gap: Expensive, requires planning ahead, doesn't use pantry

**AnyList / Paprika:**
- Job: Optimize grocery shopping
- Gap: Recipe-first, not pantry-first

**Our Advantage:** Pantry-first approach solves multiple jobs simultaneously

## MEASURING JOB SUCCESS

### Job 1: Eliminate Decision Fatigue
- **Metric:** Time to meal suggestion (<30 seconds)
- **Metric:** Decision fatigue score (survey: "How stressed do you feel deciding what to cook?")
- **Target:** 50%+ reduction in decision time

### Job 2: Reduce Food Waste
- **Metric:** Expiration alerts sent
- **Metric:** Expiring items used before expiry
- **Target:** 30%+ reduction in food waste

### Job 3: Save Time on Meal Planning
- **Metric:** Time spent meal planning (before vs after)
- **Metric:** Weekly meal plans created
- **Target:** 70%+ reduction in planning time

### Job 4: Discover New Recipes
- **Metric:** New recipes discovered per month
- **Metric:** Recipe diversity score
- **Target:** 5+ new recipes per month per user

### Job 5: Optimize Grocery Shopping
- **Metric:** Grocery trips per week
- **Metric:** Shopping list accuracy
- **Target:** 50%+ reduction in grocery trips

## USER STORIES FROM JOBS

- **As a** busy parent,  
  **I want** instant meal suggestions when I open the app,  
  **So that** I can start cooking immediately without thinking.

- **As a** tired professional,  
  **I want** meal suggestions based on what I have,

  **I want** suggestions that prioritize expiring items,  
  **So that** I don't waste money on food I throw away.

### From Job 3: Save Time on Meal Planning
- **As a** busy person,  
  **I want** AI to generate my weekly meal plan,  
  **So that** I can spend my weekend doing fun things.

  **I want** a meal plan with shopping list,  
  **So that** I can shop once and cook for the week.
