# Optimization & Profitability Guide

Complete guide to maximizing revenue, conversions, and user acquisition.

## 🎯 Revenue Optimization Strategies

### 1. Pricing Strategy

**Current Pricing:**
- Free: $0 (limited features)
- Pro: $9.99/month or $99.99/year (17% savings)
- Family: $19.99/month or $199.99/year (17% savings)

**Optimization Tips:**
- A/B test annual vs monthly pricing
- Test different price points ($8.99, $9.99, $11.99)
- Offer limited-time discounts
- Bundle deals (annual + bonus features)

### 2. Conversion Rate Optimization

**Funnel Optimization:**
1. **Landing Page:** 2-3% conversion → 5-7% target
2. **Signup:** 60% → 70% target
3. **Trial to Paid:** 15% → 25% target
4. **Referral Click to Signup:** 10% → 20% target

**Tactics:**
- Social proof (testimonials, user count)
- Urgency (limited-time offers)
- Clear value propositions
- Reduced friction (one-click signup)
- Exit-intent popups

### 3. Lead Generation

**Lead Magnets:**
- Free meal planning templates
- 7-day meal plan PDFs
- Recipe collections
- Grocery shopping guides

**Implementation:**
```tsx
<LeadMagnet
  title="Get Your Free Meal Planning Guide"
  description="7-Day Template + Grocery List System"
  offer="Free Download"
/>
```

### 4. Program Optimization

**Referral Program:**
- Current: 30 days free (both parties)
- Test: 45 days, 60 days, or $10 credit
- Track conversion rates by reward type

**Affiliate Program:**
- Current: 20% recurring
- Test: 25% for first 3 months, then 20%
- Tiered commissions (more referrals = higher %)

**Partner Program:**
- Revenue share: 5-25% based on tier
- Co-marketing opportunities
- MDF (Market Development Funds)

## 📈 A/B Testing Framework

### Tests to Run

1. **Pricing Page:**
   - Annual vs Monthly prominence
   - Price anchoring ($9.99 vs $11.99)
   - Feature comparison layout

2. **Referral Program:**
   - Reward amounts (30 vs 45 days)
   - CTA copy ("Share" vs "Invite Friends")
   - Social proof placement

3. **Affiliate Program:**
   - Commission display (% vs $)
   - Calculator defaults
   - Testimonial placement
