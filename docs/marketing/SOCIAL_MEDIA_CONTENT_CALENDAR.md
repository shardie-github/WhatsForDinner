# Social Media Content Calendar

**Platform:** Instagram, Twitter/X, Facebook, TikTok  
**Frequency:** Daily posts  
**Goal:** Drive awareness, engagement, and conversions

---

## Content Pillars

1. **Meal Ideas** (40%) - Showcase meal suggestions and recipes
2. **Tips & Hacks** (25%) - Meal planning tips, time-saving hacks
3. **User Stories** (20%) - Success stories and testimonials
4. **Behind the Scenes** (10%) - Company culture, team, product updates
5. **Promotional** (5%) - Special offers, product launches
