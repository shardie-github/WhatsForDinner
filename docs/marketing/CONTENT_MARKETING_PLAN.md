## SEO Strategy

### Target Keywords

**Primary Keywords:**
- meal planning
- meal planning app
- weekly meal planning
- meal prep ideas
- grocery list generator

**Long-Tail Keywords:**
- how to meal plan for the week
- best meal planning app
- meal planning tips for beginners
- meal planning on a budget
- meal planning for families

### Content Optimization

1. **Title Tags:** Include primary keyword, under 60 characters
2. **Meta Descriptions:** Include keyword, compelling CTA, under 160 characters
3. **Headers:** Use H1 for title, H2/H3 for sections
4. **Internal Linking:** Link to relevant app features
5. **Images:** Optimize with alt text and keywords
