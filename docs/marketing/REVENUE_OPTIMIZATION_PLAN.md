# Revenue Optimization Plan

**Goal:** Maximize revenue from day one through strategic pricing, conversion optimization, and retention

---

## Revenue Streams

### Primary Revenue Streams

1. **Premium Subscriptions** ($9.99/month)
   - Target: 5% conversion rate
   - Goal: $500/month by month 1
   - Strategy: Value-based pricing, free trial

2. **Annual Plans** ($79.99/year)
   - Target: 20% of Premium users
   - Goal: $1,600/month by month 1
   - Strategy: Discount incentive, annual commitment

3. **Enterprise Plans** (Custom pricing)
   - Target: Large families/organizations
   - Goal: $2,000+/month by month 3
   - Strategy: Custom features, dedicated support

---

## Conversion Optimization

### Free to Premium Conversion

**Current Funnel:**
1. Signup (100%)
2. First meal planned (60%)
3. Pricing page viewed (30%)
4. Upgrade started (10%)
5. Upgrade completed (5%)

**Optimization Targets:**
- Increase pricing page views: 30% → 50%
- Increase upgrade starts: 10% → 20%
- Increase upgrade completions: 5% → 10%

**Tactics:**
- In-app upgrade prompts for engaged users
- Limited-time offers for free users
- Feature gating (show Premium features)
- Social proof (show other Premium users)
- Value demonstrations (show time saved)

## Retention Strategy

### Reduce Churn

**Churn Prevention:**
- Proactive engagement for at-risk users
- Feature usage reminders
- Success stories and tips
- Special offers for returning users

**Win-Back Campaigns:**
- Email sequence for churned users
- Special discount offers
- "We miss you" messaging
- Feature updates and improvements

**Retention Targets:**
- Month 1 retention: > 80%
- Month 3 retention: > 60%
- Month 6 retention: > 40%
- Annual retention: > 30%
