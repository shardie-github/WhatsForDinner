## How It Works

### Day 1: Onboarding
1. **Quick preferences**: Dietary restrictions, cooking skill, time available
2. **Pantry scan**: Add what you have (or start fresh)
3. **First meal plan**: AI suggests 7 days of meals in 30 seconds

### Daily Use
- **Morning**: Review today's meal suggestion, adjust if needed
- **Shopping**: Grocery list auto-updates based on meal plan
- **Cooking**: Step-by-step recipe guidance with timers
- **After meal**: Rate and feedback loops improve future suggestions

### Key Integrations
- 🛒 **Grocery stores**: Loblaws, Metro, Sobeys (Canadian retailers)
- 💳 **Payment**: Stripe (CAD) with GST/HST handling
- 📧 **Notifications**: CASL-compliant email reminders (opt-in)
- ☁️ **Sync**: Works offline, syncs when online
