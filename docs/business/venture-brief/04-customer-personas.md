# Customer Personas

**What's for Dinner? — Target Customer Profiles**

## Overview

This document defines the primary and secondary customer personas for **What's for Dinner?**, based on user research, interviews, and market analysis. Each persona includes demographics, pain points, goals, and product fit.

## Primary Persona 1: "The Busy Professional" (Solo)

### Demographics
- **Age**: 28-45
- **Location**: Urban/suburban Canada (Toronto, Vancouver, Calgary, Montreal)
- **Income**: CAD $60K-$120K annually
- **Occupation**: Full-time professional (tech, consulting, finance, healthcare)
- **Living Situation**: Lives alone or with partner (no kids)
- **Tech Savviness**: High (early adopter)

### Psychographics
- **Values**: Efficiency, work-life balance, health
- **Lifestyle**: Busy schedule, works 50+ hours/week, values convenience
- **Cooking Skill**: Intermediate (can follow recipes, limited time)
- **Dietary Preferences**: Vegetarian/vegan options important, health-conscious

### Pain Points
1. **Decision Fatigue**: Spends 40+ minutes daily deciding what to cook
2. **Time Constraints**: Gets home late, too tired to plan meals
3. **Food Waste**: Buys groceries but doesn't use them, ends up ordering takeout
4. **Cost**: Spends CAD $400+ monthly on takeout/delivery
5. **Lack of Variety**: Cooks same 5-7 meals repeatedly

### Goals
- Save time on meal planning (target: <5 minutes/day)
- Reduce takeout spending (target: <CAD $200/month)
- Eat healthier meals without sacrificing convenience
- Reduce food waste and grocery spending

### Product Fit
- ✅ **High Fit**: One-tap meal planning saves time
- ✅ **Value**: Saves 12+ minutes/day = CAD $200+ monthly
- ✅ **Pricing**: Willing to pay CAD $9.99-$19.99/month
- ✅ **Usage**: Daily use, morning routine (plan day's meals)

### Messaging
> "Stop wasting 40 minutes every day deciding what to cook. Get personalized meal suggestions in 30 seconds, synced with your grocery store."

### Channels
- **Acquisition**: LinkedIn ads, Product Hunt, App Store search
- **Retention**: Push notifications (morning meal reminders), email (weekly recap)

1. **Budget Constraints**: Limited grocery budget (CAD $200-300/month)

3. **Food Waste**: Buys ingredients but doesn't use them all

5. **Lack of Cooking Knowledge**: Doesn't know how to meal plan effectively

### Goals
- Stretch grocery budget further (target: CAD $150-200/month)
- Learn to cook affordable, healthy meals
- Reduce food waste (environmental + financial)
- Save time on meal planning

### Product Fit
- ✅ **High Fit**: Solo-friendly portion sizing, budget filters
- ✅ **Value**: Saves CAD $50-100/month on groceries
- ✅ **Pricing**: Free tier sufficient, may upgrade to Starter (CAD $9.99/month)
- ✅ **Usage**: 3-4 times/week, weekend meal planning

### Messaging
> "Save CAD $200+ monthly by reducing food waste and cutting takeout. Meal planning that fits your student budget."

### Channels
- **Acquisition**: TikTok, Instagram, Reddit (r/MealPrepSunday, r/EatCheapAndHealthy)
- **Retention**: Social features (share meals with roommates), student discounts
