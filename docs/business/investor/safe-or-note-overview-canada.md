## SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity)

### What is a SAFE?

**SAFE** is a simple agreement between investor and company where:
- Investor invests cash now
- Company issues equity in the future (at conversion event)
- No interest, no maturity date (simpler than convertible notes)

### Key Terms

**Investment Amount**: CAD $50K-100K (seed round)

**Valuation Cap**: CAD $2M-5M (pre-money valuation cap)
- **Purpose**: Protects investors from overpaying if company raises at higher valuation
- **Example**: If company raises at CAD $10M valuation, SAFE converts at CAD $2M cap (better for investor)

**Discount Rate**: 20% (typical)
- **Purpose**: Rewards early investors for taking risk
- **Example**: If company raises at CAD $10/share, SAFE converts at CAD $8/share (20% discount)

**Conversion Trigger**: Next equity financing (typically CAD $500K+)
- **When**: SAFE converts to equity when company raises priced round
- **How**: Converts at lower of valuation cap or discount rate

**Pro-Rata Rights**: Optional (investor right to participate in future rounds)

## Convertible Note (Alternative)

### What is a Convertible Note?

**Convertible Note** is a loan that converts to equity:
- Investor lends cash now
- Company pays interest (typically 4-8% annually)
- Note converts to equity at conversion event
- Has maturity date (typically 18-24 months)

### Key Terms

**Principal Amount**: CAD $50K-100K

**Interest Rate**: 4-8% annually (typical)
- **Payment**: Usually accrued (not paid until conversion)
- **Example**: CAD $50K note × 6% interest = CAD $3K/year interest

**Maturity Date**: 18-24 months (typical)
- **If Not Converted**: Note becomes due (company must repay or convert)
- **Risk**: Company must repay if not converted (not ideal for startups)

**Conversion Terms**: Same as SAFE (valuation cap, discount rate)

**Conversion Trigger**: Next equity financing (typically CAD $500K+)

## Canadian Considerations

### Tax Implications

**SAFE**:
- No immediate tax implications (not equity until conversion)
- Tax on conversion (when SAFE converts to equity)

**Convertible Note**:
- Interest income (investor pays tax on interest)
- Tax on conversion (when note converts to equity)

**Advice**: Consult Canadian tax advisor before finalizing

### Legal Structure

**SAFE**:
- Not a loan (no interest, no maturity)
- Not equity (until conversion)
- Contractual agreement (future equity)

**Convertible Note**:
- Loan agreement (with interest, maturity)
- Converts to equity (at conversion event)

**Advice**: Consult Canadian legal counsel before finalizing
