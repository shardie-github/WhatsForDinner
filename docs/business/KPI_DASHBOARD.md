## Automated Alerts

### Critical KPI Alerts
- Sent via email when KPIs fall below 50% of target
- Sent to: `KPI_ALERT_EMAILS` environment variable
- Frequency: Hourly checks

### Alert Types
1. **Critical:** <50% of target → Immediate action required
2. **Warning:** 50-80% of target → Monitor closely
3. **Info:** 80-120% of target → On track
4. **Exceeding:** >120% of target → Celebrate! 🎉
