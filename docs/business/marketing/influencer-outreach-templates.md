## Best Practices

### Personalization
- ✅ Research influencer (read their content, understand their audience)
- ✅ Mention specific content (show you've done your homework)
- ✅ Personalize subject line, opening line

### Value Proposition
- ✅ Clearly state what's in it for them (free access, commission, collaboration)
- ✅ Highlight benefits (Canadian focus, grocery integration)
- ✅ Provide social proof (user count, ratings, testimonials)

### Follow-Up
- ✅ Follow up 1-2 weeks after initial email
- ✅ Be respectful (don't spam, don't push)
- ✅ Provide value (offer something useful)
