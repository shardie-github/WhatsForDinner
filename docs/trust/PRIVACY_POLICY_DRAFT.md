## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

- **Account Information:** Name, email address, password (hashed)
- **Pantry Data:** Ingredients you add, dietary preferences, allergies
- **Usage Data:** Recipes you save, meal plans you create, feedback you provide
- **Payment Information:** Processed by Stripe (we don't store full card details)

### 2.2 Information Automatically Collected

- **Device Information:** Browser type, operating system, device identifiers
- **Usage Data:** Pages visited, time spent, clicks, interactions
- **Location Data:** General location (country/city) inferred from IP address
- **Cookies & Tracking:** Session cookies, analytics cookies (with consent)

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties

- **Authentication Providers:** If you sign in with Google/Apple, we receive basic profile information
- **Payment Processors:** Stripe provides transaction status and subscription details

## 8. Your Rights (Data Subject Rights)

### 8.1 Right to Access

Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.

**How:** [Export My Data](/account/export) or email privacy@whatsfordinner.com  
**Timeline:** Within 30 days

### 8.2 Right to Rectification

Correct inaccurate personal data.

**How:** Edit your profile or email privacy@whatsfordinner.com  
**Timeline:** Within 7 days

### 8.3 Right to Erasure

Request deletion of your account and data.

**How:** [Delete Account](/settings/account/delete) or email privacy@whatsfordinner.com  
**Timeline:** Within 30 days  
**Note:** Some data may be retained for legal compliance

### 8.4 Right to Data Portability

Export your data in a machine-readable format.

**How:** [Export My Data](/account/export)  
**Formats:** JSON, CSV  
**Timeline:** Within 30 days

### 8.5 Right to Object

Object to processing for certain purposes (e.g., marketing).

**How:** Privacy settings or email privacy@whatsfordinner.com  
**Timeline:** Within 7 days

### 8.6 Right to Restrict Processing

Request restriction of data processing in certain circumstances.

**How:** Email privacy@whatsfordinner.com  
**Timeline:** Within 7 days

### 8.7 Right to Withdraw Consent

Withdraw consent for analytics or marketing at any time.

**How:** Privacy settings or email privacy@whatsfordinner.com  
**Timeline:** Immediate
