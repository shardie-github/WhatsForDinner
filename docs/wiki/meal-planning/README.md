# Meal Planning Guide

Learn how to create, customize, and manage your meal plans with What's for Dinner.

---

## 📅 Creating Meal Plans

### Quick Plan (Recommended)

1. **Start Planning**
   - Go to "Meal Planner" in the menu
   - Click "Plan My Week"
   - AI analyzes your pantry and preferences

2. **Review Suggestions**
   - See personalized meal recommendations
   - Each suggestion includes:
     - Recipe details
     - Cooking time
     - Ingredients needed
     - Nutrition info

3. **Build Your Plan**
   - Tap meals to add to your plan
   - Drag to rearrange days
   - Swipe to see more options

### Custom Plan

1. **Manual Planning**
   - Click "Create Custom Plan"
   - Choose meals from your favorites
   - Or search the recipe library

2. **Mix & Match**
   - Combine AI suggestions with manual picks
   - Use "What's in my fridge?" for inspiration
   - Plan around leftovers

## 🎯 Weekly Planning

### Planning for the Week

**Sunday Planning (Recommended)**
- Plan your week on Sunday
- Generate grocery list
- Shop once for the week
- Save time all week long

**Step-by-Step:**
1. Open Meal Planner
2. Select "Plan This Week"
3. Review AI suggestions
4. Add meals to each day
5. Generate shopping list
6. Shop or order online

### Daily Planning

**Quick Daily Plans**
- Plan individual days anytime
- Use "Plan Tonight" for quick suggestions
- Based on what's in your pantry

## 🤖 AI Meal Suggestions

### How AI Suggestions Work

The AI considers:
- **Your Pantry:** What ingredients you have
- **Preferences:** Dietary restrictions, favorite cuisines
- **History:** Recipes you've loved before
- **Time:** Available cooking time
- **Goals:** Health goals, budget constraints

### Improving Suggestions

**Rate Recipes**
- Thumbs up meals you love
- Thumbs down ones you don't
- AI learns your preferences

**Update Preferences**
- Go to Settings → Preferences
- Update dietary restrictions
- Adjust favorite cuisines

## 📝 Managing Your Plan

### Editing Your Plan

**Change Meals**
- Tap any meal in your plan
- Click "Replace" or "Remove"
- Choose a new meal

**Reorganize Days**
- Drag meals between days
- Long-press to move
- Auto-saves changes

### Planning Features

**Duplicate Meals**
- Plan the same meal multiple times
- Great for meal prep
- Batch cooking friendly

**Skip Days**
- Leave days blank if needed
- Plan around events
- Flexible scheduling

## ❓ Common Questions

**Q: Can I plan just dinners?**  
A: Yes! You can plan breakfast, lunch, dinner, or snacks individually.

**Q: How far in advance can I plan?**  
A: Plan up to 4 weeks ahead, or as far as you like!

**Q: Can I reuse old meal plans?**  
A: Yes! Save plans as templates and reuse them anytime.

**Q: What if I don't cook a planned meal?**  
A: No problem! Skip it, move it, or remove it. The plan is flexible.
