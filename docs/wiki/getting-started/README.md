# Getting Started with What's for Dinner

Welcome to What's for Dinner! This guide will help you get started with meal planning in just a few minutes.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start (5 Minutes)

### Step 1: Create Your Account

1. **Sign Up**
   - Click "Sign Up" on the homepage
   - Use your email or sign in with Google/Apple
   - Verify your email (check your inbox)

2. **Complete Your Profile**
   - Tell us your dietary preferences
   - Set your family size
   - Choose your cooking skill level

### Step 2: Set Up Your Pantry

1. **Add Ingredients**
   - Go to "Pantry" in the main menu
   - Click "Add Item" or scan barcodes
   - Add common items you always have

2. **Enable Smart Pantry**
   - Let the app learn your common items
   - Get automatic expiration tracking
   - Receive low-stock alerts

### Step 3: Create Your First Meal Plan

1. **Get AI Suggestions**
   - Click "Meal Planner" in the menu
   - Tap "Plan My Week"
   - AI will suggest meals based on your pantry

2. **Customize Your Plan**
   - Swipe through suggestions
   - Tap to add meals to your plan
   - Adjust servings for your family

3. **Generate Grocery List**
   - Once your plan is ready
   - Click "Generate Shopping List"
   - Missing ingredients are automatically added

## 💡 Pro Tips for New Users

- **Start Small:** Plan just 3-4 meals your first week
- **Use Smart Pantry:** Let the app learn what you keep in stock
- **Save Favorites:** Build a collection of recipes you love
- **Set Reminders:** Enable notifications for meal planning

---

## ❓ Common First-Time Questions

**Q: Do I need to add everything in my pantry?**  
A: No! Start with common items. The app will learn over time.

**Q: Can I change my meal plan after creating it?**  
A: Yes! You can edit, swap, or remove meals anytime.

**Q: What if I don't like a suggested recipe?**  
A: Swipe to see more options, or search for specific recipes.

**Q: How do I get the app on my phone?**  
A: Visit the App Store (iOS) or Play Store (Android) and search "What's for Dinner".
