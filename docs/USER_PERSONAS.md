## PRIMARY PERSONA: "Busy Parent" (Sarah)

### Demographics
- **Age:** 32
- **Location:** Suburban Midwest
- **Household:** Married, 2 kids (ages 5 and 8)
- **Income:** $75K household
- **Education:** Bachelor's degree
- **Occupation:** Marketing manager (works 9-5)

### Psychographics
- **Values:** Family time, convenience, health (but practical)
- **Frustrations:** 
  - Decision fatigue ("What's for dinner?" every single day)
  - Time constraints (works full-time, kids' activities)
  - Food waste (buys ingredients, forgets what she has)
  - Picky eaters (kids won't eat certain things)
- **Goals:**
  - Quick, easy meals the whole family will eat
  - Use what she already has (reduce waste)
  - Spend less time meal planning
  - Feel confident in the kitchen

### Tech Profile
- **Devices:** iPhone 13, MacBook Air, iPad (kids use)
- **Apps:** Instagram, Amazon, Target app, banking apps
- **Tech Comfort:** Medium (uses apps daily but not an early adopter)
- **Shopping:** Mostly online (Amazon, Target), occasional in-store

### Daily Routine
- **6:30 AM:** Wake up, get kids ready
- **7:30 AM:** Drop kids at school
- **8:00 AM:** Work starts
- **5:00 PM:** Pick up kids
- **5:30 PM:** **THE STRUGGLE** - "What's for dinner?"
- **6:00 PM:** Start cooking (or order takeout if no plan)
- **7:00 PM:** Dinner with family
- **8:00 PM:** Kids' bedtime routine
- **9:00 PM:** Personal time (TV, social media)

### Pain Points
1. **6 PM Decision Fatigue**
   - Stares at fridge, no idea what to make
   - Too tired to think creatively
   - Kids are hungry NOW

2. **Food Waste**
   - Buys ingredients with good intentions
   - Forgets what she has
   - Ingredients expire before use
   - Feels guilty about waste

3. **Time Constraints**
   - Doesn't have time to meal plan on weekends
   - Needs quick meals (<30 minutes)
   - Can't spend hours cooking

4. **Picky Eaters**
   - Kids won't eat certain foods
   - Needs family-friendly recipes
   - Wants variety but not too adventurous

### How We Help
- **Instant Suggestions:** Get meal ideas in 30 seconds, not 30 minutes
- **Pantry-First:** Start with what she has, not what she needs
- **Family-Friendly:** Filter recipes by kid-friendly options
- **Quick Meals:** Prioritize recipes under 30 minutes
- **Reduce Waste:** Highlight expiring ingredients first

### User Journey
1. **Discovery:** Sees ad on Instagram or App Store
2. **Signup:** Downloads app, creates account
3. **Onboarding:** Adds 5 pantry items, sets preferences (family-friendly, quick meals)
4. **First Use:** Gets suggestion at 5:30 PM, makes it, kids love it
5. **Habit:** Uses app daily at 5:30 PM for dinner ideas
6. **Expansion:** Starts meal planning for the week
7. **Advocacy:** Tells friends about the app

### Success Metrics
- Uses app 5+ times per week
- Creates meal plan for the week
- Reduces food waste by 30%+
- Saves 2+ hours per week on meal planning

- **Household:** Single, lives alone
- **Income:** $55K
- **Education:** Bachelor's degree
- **Occupation:** Software engineer (works remotely)

### Psychographics
- **Values:** Health, efficiency, variety, cost-saving
- **Frustrations:**
  - Wants to meal prep but doesn't know where to start
  - Gets bored with same recipes
  - Wastes ingredients buying too much
  - Doesn't have time to research recipes
- **Goals:**
  - Discover new, healthy recipes
  - Optimize grocery shopping
  - Meal prep efficiently for the week
  - Save money on food

### Tech Profile
- **Devices:** iPhone 14 Pro, MacBook Pro, Apple Watch
- **Apps:** Reddit, Twitter, Spotify, MyFitnessPal, banking apps
- **Tech Comfort:** High (early adopter, loves trying new apps)
- **Shopping:** Mix of online (Amazon Fresh) and in-store (Trader Joe's)

### Daily Routine
- **7:00 AM:** Wake up, coffee, check phone
- **8:00 AM:** Work starts (remote)
- **12:00 PM:** Lunch (usually meal prep from Sunday)
- **5:00 PM:** Work ends
- **6:00 PM:** Gym or run
- **7:00 PM:** Dinner (cook or meal prep)
- **8:00 PM:** Personal projects, social media
- **11:00 PM:** Bed

### Pain Points
1. **Recipe Discovery**
   - Spends hours browsing recipes online
   - Hard to find recipes that use what he has
   - Wants variety but doesn't know where to start

2. **Meal Prep Planning**
   - Doesn't know how to plan meals for the week
   - Overbuys ingredients
   - Wastes food because he buys too much

3. **Grocery Shopping**
   - Makes multiple trips per week
   - Forgets items
   - Spends too much money

4. **Cooking Skills**
   - Intermediate cook but wants to improve
   - Wants to try new cuisines
   - Needs guidance on meal prep techniques

### How We Help
- **Recipe Discovery:** AI suggests recipes based on pantry and preferences
- **Meal Prep Mode:** Plan entire week, generate shopping list
- **Grocery Optimization:** Smart shopping lists, avoid overbuying
- **Variety:** Discover new cuisines and recipes
- **Efficiency:** Save time on meal planning

### User Journey
1. **Discovery:** Sees Reddit post about meal prep apps
2. **Signup:** Downloads app, creates account
3. **Onboarding:** Adds pantry items, sets preferences (healthy, meal prep-friendly)
4. **First Use:** Gets meal suggestions, creates meal plan for the week
5. **Habit:** Uses app weekly to plan meals and generate shopping lists
6. **Expansion:** Explores new cuisines, saves favorite recipes
7. **Advocacy:** Shares meal prep results on Reddit

### Success Metrics
- Creates weekly meal plans
- Reduces grocery trips by 50%+
- Discovers 5+ new recipes per month
- Saves $50+ per month on groceries
