## 🎨 Brand Identity

### Brand Archetype
**Primary:** Caregiver (warmth, trust, support)  
**Secondary:** Joyful Simplifier (efficiency with delight)

### Core Promise
**"Dinner plans that plan themselves—so you can reclaim your evenings."**

### Brand Values
1. **Empowerment** - We give users control and confidence
2. **Simplicity** - Complex made simple, always
3. **Joy** - Meal planning should be delightful, not stressful
4. **Intelligence** - AI that actually learns and adapts
5. **Trust** - Privacy-first, user data is sacred

## 💬 Tone of Voice

### Core Traits

1. **Warm but Competent**
   - Friendly without being casual
   - Confident without being arrogant
   - Example: "We've got your back"

2. **Conversational**
   - "Let's plan dinner together" not "Please utilize our meal planning system"
   - Natural language, not corporate jargon

3. **Empowering**
   - "You've got this" vs. "We'll do it for you"
   - User is in control, we're the guide

4. **Joyful**
   - Subtle humor, not jokes
   - "Dinner's ready. (Well, the plan is.)"
   - Lighthearted, not silly

### Voice Examples

**✅ Good:**
- "Dinner plans that plan themselves."
- "3 meals planned. 3 evenings saved. You're welcome."
- "Your dinner, done smarter. No cape required."

**❌ Bad:**
- "Optimize your meal planning workflow"
- "Enhance your culinary efficiency"
- "Revolutionary meal planning paradigm"

## 🚫 Brand Don'ts

1. **Don't use:**
   - Corporate jargon
   - Technical terms without explanation
   - Manipulative urgency
   - Dark patterns

2. **Don't say:**
   - "Revolutionary" (overused)
   - "Game-changing" (cliché)
   - "Only X spots left!" (fake scarcity)

3. **Don't show:**
   - Perfect, unrealistic meals
   - Overly staged photos
   - Exclusivity messaging

---

## ✅ Brand Do's

1. **Do use:**
   - Real user stories
   - Authentic testimonials
   - Clear, honest communication
   - Helpful, educational content

2. **Do say:**
   - "Here's how it works"
   - "Real users say..."
   - "Try it free"
   - "No credit card required"

3. **Do show:**
   - Real kitchen photos
   - Diverse families
   - Honest results
   - Process transparency
