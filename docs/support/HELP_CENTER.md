## Getting Started

### Quick Start Guide

**Welcome to What's For Dinner!** Here's how to get started in 3 simple steps:

1. **Sign Up** (30 seconds)
   - Create your free account
   - No credit card required
   - Start immediately

2. **Add Your Pantry** (2 minutes)
   - Tell us what ingredients you have
   - Add dietary preferences
   - Set cooking time preferences

3. **Get Meal Suggestions** (30 seconds)
   - Click "Generate Meals"
   - Browse AI-powered suggestions
   - Select meals for your week

**That's it!** You're ready to start planning better meals.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Account & Billing

**Q: Is What's For Dinner really free?**  
A: Yes! Our free plan includes 5 meal suggestions per week, basic meal planning, and grocery list generation. No credit card required.

**Q: What's included in Premium?**  
A: Premium includes unlimited meal suggestions, advanced nutrition tracking, family collaboration, recipe customization, meal prep guides, calendar integration, and priority support.

**Q: Can I cancel anytime?**  
A: Absolutely! Cancel anytime from your account settings. No questions asked, and you'll continue to have access until the end of your billing period.

**Q: Do you offer refunds?**  
A: Yes! We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied, contact us for a full refund.

**Q: How do I upgrade to Premium?**  
A: Go to Settings → Billing → Upgrade. You can start a free 7-day trial, no credit card required.

---

### Using the App

**Q: How does AI meal suggestion work?**  
A: Our AI analyzes your pantry items, dietary preferences, cooking time, and past preferences to suggest personalized meals. The more you use it, the better it gets!

**Q: Can I customize meal suggestions?**  
A: Yes! You can filter by cuisine, dietary restrictions, cooking time, and more. Premium users can also customize recipes.

**Q: How do I add items to my pantry?**  
A: Go to Pantry → Add Items. You can type ingredients, scan barcodes (mobile), or import from recipes.

**Q: Can I share meal plans with my family?**  
A: Yes! Premium users can invite family members to collaborate on meal plans. Everyone can see what's for dinner and add preferences.

**Q: How do I generate a grocery list?**  
A: After planning your meals, go to Grocery Lists → Generate List. The app automatically creates an organized shopping list.

**Q: Can I track nutrition?**  
A: Yes! Premium users get detailed nutrition tracking including calories, macros (protein, carbs, fat), vitamins, and minerals.

---

### Technical Support

**Q: Is there a mobile app?**  
A: Yes! Download our mobile app for iOS and Android. All features work on mobile.

**Q: Can I use the app offline?**  
A: Basic features work offline. Meal suggestions require an internet connection.

**Q: How do I reset my password?**  
A: Go to Sign In → Forgot Password. Enter your email and we'll send a reset link.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**  
A: Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. We'll delete all your data within 30 days.

**Q: Is my data secure?**  
A: Yes! We use industry-standard encryption and never share your data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Problem: Not receiving meal suggestions**  
**Solution:**
1. Check your internet connection
2. Ensure you have pantry items added
3. Try refreshing the page
4. Contact support if issue persists

**Problem: Grocery list not generating**  
**Solution:**
1. Make sure you have meals planned
2. Check that meals have ingredients
3. Try regenerating the list
4. Contact support if issue persists

**Problem: App is slow**  
**Solution:**
1. Clear your browser cache
2. Try a different browser
3. Check your internet connection
4. Update the app if using mobile

**Problem: Can't sign in**  
**Solution:**
1. Check your email and password
2. Try resetting your password
3. Clear browser cookies
4. Contact support if issue persists

### Meal Planning

**How to Plan Your Week:**
1. Go to Meal Planner
2. Select days you want to plan
3. Click "Generate Suggestions"
4. Browse and select meals
5. Save your plan
