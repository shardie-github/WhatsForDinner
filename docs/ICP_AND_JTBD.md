# Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs) & Jobs To Be Done (JTBD)

**Last Updated**: 2025-01-09  
**Status**: Initial Analysis - Requires Validation

---

## Executive Summary

This document defines 5 concrete Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs) for "What's for Dinner?" and maps their Jobs To Be Done (JTBD) to product capabilities. Each ICP includes specific demographics, pain points, value propositions, and measurable outcomes.

**Key Insight**: The product solves the universal "what's for dinner?" problem, but different segments experience this problem differently—from time poverty to budget constraints to health goals.

---

## ICP #1: The Time-Poor Professional

### Demographics
- **Age**: 28-45
- **Income**: $60k-$120k/year
- **Location**: Urban/suburban
- **Household**: Solo or couple (no kids)
- **Tech Savviness**: High (uses 5+ apps daily)
- **Cooking Frequency**: 3-5 times/week
- **Grocery Budget**: $200-$400/month

### Day in the Life

**6:30 AM**: Wake up, check phone, rush to get ready  
**7:30 AM**: Commute (30-45 min), thinking about work  
**9:00 AM - 6:00 PM**: Work day, back-to-back meetings, lunch at desk  
**6:30 PM**: Finally home, exhausted, opens fridge  
**6:45 PM**: Stares at ingredients, feels decision fatigue  
**7:00 PM**: Either orders takeout ($25-35) or makes something uninspired  
**7:30 PM**: Eats while watching Netflix, feels guilty about waste/spending

**Pain Points**:
- Decision fatigue after 10-hour workday
- Wastes $200-300/month on takeout when pantry has food
- Throws away expired food because forgot what they bought
- Spends 20+ minutes deciding what to cook
- Feels guilty about not cooking "properly"

### The Problem This Product Solves

**Primary Pain**: "I'm too tired to decide what to cook, so I order takeout even though I have food at home."

**Value of Solving It**:
- **Time**: Saves 20 min/day deciding = 10 hours/month
- **Money**: Reduces takeout by 50% = $100-150/month saved
- **Stress**: Eliminates decision fatigue at 6 PM
- **Food Waste**: Reduces waste by 30% = $30-50/month saved

**Total Value**: ~$180-200/month + 10 hours + reduced stress

### Jobs To Be Done

#### JTBD 1.1: Decision Support
**When I** get home at 6:30 PM exhausted, **I want to** see 3 meal suggestions based on what's in my fridge, **so I can** start cooking in under 2 minutes without thinking.

**How Product Addresses**: ✅ AI meal suggestions based on pantry inventory, sorted by "can make now"

#### JTBD 1.2: Pantry Visibility
**When I** open my fridge and see random ingredients, **I want to** know what meals I can actually make right now, **so I can** avoid ordering takeout unnecessarily.

**How Product Addresses**: ✅ Pantry tracking with "recipes you can make" filter

#### JTBD 1.3: Waste Prevention
**When I** buy groceries on Sunday, **I want to** get alerts before food expires, **so I can** use it before it goes bad.

**How Product Addresses**: ✅ Expiration tracking with alerts (needs validation - may need to build)

#### JTBD 1.4: Quick Shopping
**When I** realize I'm missing 2 ingredients for tonight's meal, **I want to** add them to a shopping list and order via Instacart in one click, **so I can** get groceries delivered in 30 minutes.

**How Product Addresses**: ⚠️ Shopping list exists, but grocery integration is "coming soon" - **GAP**

#### JTBD 1.5: Meal Planning Automation
**When I** have 30 minutes on Sunday, **I want to** generate a week's meal plan automatically, **so I can** shop once and not think about dinner all week.

**How Product Addresses**: ✅ AI meal planning exists, but needs validation on quality/usability

## ICP #2: The Busy Parent

### Demographics
- **Age**: 32-45
- **Income**: $80k-$150k/year
- **Location**: Suburban
- **Household**: 2 adults + 2-3 kids (ages 5-15)
- **Tech Savviness**: Medium-High
- **Cooking Frequency**: 6-7 times/week (every night)
- **Grocery Budget**: $600-$900/month

### Day in the Life

**6:00 AM**: Wake up, pack lunches, get kids ready  
**7:30 AM**: Drop kids at school  
**8:00 AM - 5:00 PM**: Work  
**5:30 PM**: Pick up kids, drive to activities  
**6:00 PM**: Home, kids are hungry NOW  
**6:15 PM**: Frantically searches fridge, kids complaining  
**6:30 PM**: Makes something quick but not nutritious  
**7:00 PM**: Kids eat, one kid doesn't like it  
**7:30 PM**: Makes second meal for picky eater  
**8:00 PM**: Cleans up, exhausted, feels like a failure

**Pain Points**:
- Needs to feed 4-5 people every single night
- Kids have different preferences/allergies
- Runs out of ideas after 2 weeks of rotation
- Spends $800/month on groceries but still orders pizza 2x/week
- Feels guilty about not planning better meals
- Wastes food because kids won't eat leftovers

### The Problem This Product Solves

**Primary Pain**: "I need to feed my family nutritious meals every night, but I'm out of ideas and don't have time to plan."

**Value of Solving It**:
- **Time**: Saves 30 min/day on meal planning = 15 hours/month
- **Money**: Reduces last-minute takeout by 60% = $120-180/month saved
- **Stress**: Eliminates "what's for dinner?" panic at 6 PM
- **Family Harmony**: Reduces complaints about repetitive meals
- **Nutrition**: Improves meal variety and nutrition

**Total Value**: ~$150-200/month + 15 hours + reduced family stress

### Jobs To Be Done

#### JTBD 2.1: Family Meal Planning
**When I** sit down on Sunday to plan the week, **I want to** generate 7 dinner ideas that account for my kids' allergies and preferences, **so I can** shop once and have peace of mind all week.

**How Product Addresses**: ⚠️ Dietary preferences exist, but family planning is "coming soon" - **GAP**

#### JTBD 2.2: Quick Kid-Friendly Meals
**When I** get home at 6 PM with hungry kids, **I want to** see 3 kid-approved meal options I can make in 20 minutes, **so I can** feed them without complaints.

**How Product Addresses**: ✅ Can filter by cooking time and dietary preferences (needs "kid-friendly" tag validation)

#### JTBD 2.3: Pantry-to-Meal Translation
**When I** have chicken, rice, and broccoli in the fridge, **I want to** see 5 different ways to combine them, **so I can** avoid making the same stir-fry every week.

**How Product Addresses**: ✅ AI meal suggestions based on pantry - **STRONG MATCH**

#### JTBD 2.4: Shopping List Coordination
**When I** plan meals for the week, **I want to** automatically generate a consolidated shopping list, **so I can** shop once instead of making 3 trips.

**How Product Addresses**: ✅ Shopping list generation exists - **STRONG MATCH**

#### JTBD 2.5: Leftover Transformation
**When I** have leftover chicken from last night, **I want to** see recipes that use it in a different way, **so I can** avoid complaints about "leftovers again."

**How Product Addresses**: ⚠️ Not explicitly supported - could work if AI suggestions include leftover ingredients - **NEEDS VALIDATION**

## ICP #3: The Health-Conscious Individual

### Demographics
- **Age**: 25-40
- **Income**: $50k-$100k/year
- **Location**: Urban/suburban
- **Household**: Solo or couple
- **Tech Savviness**: High
- **Cooking Frequency**: 5-7 times/week
- **Grocery Budget**: $300-$500/month
- **Special Characteristics**: Tracks macros, follows specific diet (keto/vegan/paleo), meal preps

### Day in the Life

**6:00 AM**: Morning workout, checks macros from yesterday  
**7:00 AM**: Meal prep for lunch (prepped Sunday)  
**8:00 AM - 5:00 PM**: Work, eats prepped lunch  
**5:30 PM**: Gym  
**6:30 PM**: Home, needs dinner that fits macros  
**7:00 PM**: Spends 15 min finding recipe that fits diet + macros  
**7:30 PM**: Cooks, weighs ingredients, tracks in app  
**8:00 PM**: Eats, logs everything  
**8:30 PM**: Realizes macros are off, feels frustrated

**Pain Points**:
- Spends 20+ min/day finding recipes that fit macros
- Struggles to hit protein targets without going over calories
- Gets bored with same 10 recipes
- Wastes time calculating nutrition for new recipes
- Feels restricted by diet, wants more variety

### The Problem This Product Solves

**Primary Pain**: "I want to eat healthy meals that fit my macros, but finding and planning them takes too much time."

**Value of Solving It**:
- **Time**: Saves 20 min/day on recipe research = 10 hours/month
- **Nutrition**: Improves macro adherence by 30%
- **Variety**: Discovers 2-3 new compliant recipes/week
- **Stress**: Reduces anxiety about hitting macros

**Total Value**: ~10 hours/month + improved health outcomes + reduced stress

### Jobs To Be Done

#### JTBD 3.1: Macro-Aligned Meal Discovery
**When I** need dinner that fits my 40g protein / 500 cal target, **I want to** see recipes filtered by macros, **so I can** choose without manual calculation.

**How Product Addresses**: ❌ Nutrition tracking is "coming soon" - **MAJOR GAP**

#### JTBD 3.2: Diet-Compliant Suggestions
**When I** follow a keto diet, **I want to** only see keto-compliant meal suggestions, **so I can** avoid accidentally choosing non-compliant recipes.

**How Product Addresses**: ✅ Dietary preferences filtering exists - **STRONG MATCH**

#### JTBD 3.3: Pantry-to-Macro Translation
**When I** have chicken and vegetables in my pantry, **I want to** see how to combine them to hit my protein goal, **so I can** avoid overeating or undereating.

**How Product Addresses**: ❌ Requires nutrition data - **GAP**

#### JTBD 3.4: Meal Prep Planning
**When I** meal prep on Sunday, **I want to** generate 5 recipes that can be batch-cooked and stored, **so I can** save time during the week.

**How Product Addresses**: ⚠️ Could work with meal planning, but "meal prep" tag/filter not explicit - **NEEDS VALIDATION**

#### JTBD 3.5: Ingredient Substitution
**When I** find a recipe I like but it's not diet-compliant, **I want to** see AI-suggested substitutions, **so I can** adapt it to my diet.

**How Product Addresses**: ⚠️ AI could potentially do this, but not explicitly supported - **NEEDS VALIDATION**

## Appendix: JTBD Format Reference

All Jobs To Be Done follow this format:

**When I** [situation/context], **I want to** [motivation/goal], **so I can** [expected outcome/benefit].

This format ensures:
- **Specificity**: Concrete situations, not vague desires
- **Measurability**: Can test if product solves the job
- **Falsifiability**: Can prove if job is NOT being done
