## METRICS FRAMEWORK

### North Star Metric
**Weekly Active Users (WAU)** - Users who get at least one meal suggestion per week

**Why:** Measures product value delivery. If users aren't getting suggestions weekly, they're not getting value.

### Key Metrics by Category

#### Acquisition Metrics
- **Signups:** Total users who create account
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** Cost to acquire one user
- **CAC by Channel:** CAC for each acquisition channel
- **Signup Source:** Where users come from (organic, paid, referral)

#### Activation Metrics
- **Activation Rate:** % of signups who get first meal suggestion
- **Time to Activation:** Time from signup to first suggestion
- **Onboarding Completion:** % who complete 5-step onboarding

#### Engagement Metrics
- **DAU (Daily Active Users):** Users active per day
- **WAU (Weekly Active Users):** Users active per week
- **MAU (Monthly Active Users):** Users active per month
- **Suggestions per Week:** Average meal suggestions per active user
- **Recipes Saved:** Average recipes saved per user

#### Retention Metrics
- **D1 Retention:** % of users active 1 day after signup
- **D7 Retention:** % of users active 7 days after signup
- **D30 Retention:** % of users active 30 days after signup
- **Churn Rate:** % of users who stop using product monthly

#### Conversion Metrics
- **Free-to-Paid Conversion:** % of free users who upgrade to premium
- **Trial-to-Paid Conversion:** % of trial users who convert
- **Annual Plan Conversion:** % of premium users on annual plan

#### Revenue Metrics
- **MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue):** Monthly subscription revenue
- **ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue):** MRR × 12
- **ARPU (Average Revenue Per User):** MRR / paying users
- **LTV (Lifetime Value):** Average revenue per user over lifetime

#### Quality Metrics
- **Suggestion Rating:** Average star rating for meal suggestions
- **Recipe Completion Rate:** % of viewed recipes that are cooked
- **Error Rate:** % of user actions that fail
- **NPS (Net Promoter Score):** User satisfaction score (-100 to +100)

## FORECAST ASSUMPTIONS & RISKS

### Key Assumptions
1. **20% MoM Growth:** Achievable with content marketing + referrals
2. **10% Conversion Rate:** Industry standard for freemium apps
3. **5% Monthly Churn:** Low churn due to daily habit formation
4. **$10 CAC:** Achievable with organic + content marketing focus
5. **$9.99/month Pricing:** Validated through pricing tests

### Risks to Forecast
1. **Lower Growth:** If growth <20% MoM → Extend timeline, increase marketing
2. **Lower Conversion:** If conversion <10% → Improve paywall, test pricing
3. **Higher Churn:** If churn >5% → Improve product quality, fix pain points
4. **Higher CAC:** If CAC >$10 → Focus on organic channels, optimize ads
5. **Lower Pricing:** If users won't pay $9.99 → Test $7.99, improve value prop

### Scenario Planning

**Best Case (Optimistic):**
