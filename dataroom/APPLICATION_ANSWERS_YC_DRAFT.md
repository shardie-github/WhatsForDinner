## What does your company do?

**Draft Answer**:

## What do you understand about your business that other companies in it just don't get?

**Draft Answer**:

> The key insight: starting with what users HAVE (not what they need) eliminates planning fatigue. Competitors optimize for search (recipe-first) or planning (future-focused), but no one optimized for "what can I make RIGHT NOW with what I have." This pantry-first approach, combined with AI that learns preferences, creates a data moat and switching cost competitors can't match.

**See**: `/yc/YC_DEFENSIBILITY_NOTES.md` for competitive advantages
