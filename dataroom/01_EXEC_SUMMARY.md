## The Problem

Every day at 6 PM, 50 million Americans face the same question: "What should I cook tonight?" This daily decision creates:
- **Decision fatigue**: 15+ minutes wasted deciding
- **Food waste**: 40% of food wasted in US households
- **Takeout dependency**: Defaulting to expensive delivery when overwhelmed

**Current solutions fail** because they're recipe-first (require searching) or planning-first (require advance planning). None start with what you actually have.

---

## Our Solution

**What's for Dinner** is an AI-powered meal planning app that:
- **Starts with your pantry** (pantry-first approach)
- **Generates personalized recipes in 30 seconds** (AI-powered)
- **Learns your preferences** (gets smarter with use)
- **Works everywhere** (web + iOS + Android)

**The more you use it, the smarter it gets** - creating a data moat and switching cost.
