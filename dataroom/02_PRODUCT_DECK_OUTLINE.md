## Slide 4: Our Solution

**What's for Dinner: Pantry-first meal planning with AI**

**How it works**:
1. User adds pantry (scan, manual, receipt)
2. AI analyzes pantry + preferences + constraints
3. System generates 3-5 personalized recipes in 30 seconds
4. User picks recipe, adds missing ingredients to shopping list
5. System learns from feedback
