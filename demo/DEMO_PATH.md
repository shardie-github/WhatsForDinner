# Demo Path: Happy Path Demo

**Purpose**: Exact steps to run a "happy path" demo from fresh user to "aha" moment  
**Time**: ~3-5 minutes

---

## Pre-Demo Setup

### Environment Check

- [ ] App running locally (`pnpm dev:web`) OR production URL ready
- [ ] Test account created (or use signup flow)
- [ ] OpenAI API key configured (for recipe generation)
- [ ] Supabase database connected

**See**: `/demo/DEMO_CHECKLIST.md` for detailed pre-demo checklist

## Demo Flow

### Step 1: Landing Page (30 seconds)

**What to Show**:
- Clean, modern landing page
- Clear value proposition: "Stop wondering. Start cooking."
- "Get Started" button

**What to Say**:
> "This is What's for Dinner. Every day at 6 PM, millions of people stare into their fridge and ask 'What's for dinner?' We solve that."

**Key Points**:
- Problem is universal
- Solution is simple

---

### Step 2: Sign Up (30 seconds)

**What to Show**:
- Quick signup form (email or social login)
- Fast, frictionless signup

**What to Say**:
> "Signup takes 30 seconds. You can use email or social login."

**Key Points**:
- Low friction
- Multiple options

---

### Step 3: Onboarding - Add Pantry (1 minute)

**What to Show**:
- Pantry management interface
- Options: Scan barcode, manual entry, receipt import
- Add 3-5 common ingredients (e.g., chicken, rice, vegetables)

**What to Say**:
> "First, we learn what you have. You can scan barcodes, enter manually, or import from receipts. Let me add a few items: chicken, rice, some vegetables."

**Key Points**:
- Pantry-first approach (differentiator)
- Multiple input methods
- Fast setup

---

### Step 4: Generate First Recipe (30 seconds)

**What to Show**:
- Click "What's for dinner?" button
- AI generates 3-5 recipe suggestions
- Recipes use ingredients from pantry

**What to Say**:
> "Now, the magic. I click 'What's for dinner?' and in 30 seconds, we get personalized recipe suggestions using what I have. No searching. No planning ahead. Just instant suggestions."

**Key Points**:
- Fast (30 seconds)
- Personalized (uses pantry)
- No planning required

---

### Step 5: Recipe Selection (30 seconds)

**What to Show**:
- Recipe detail page
- Ingredients list (shows what you have vs what you need)
- Cooking instructions
- "Add missing ingredients to shopping list" button

**What to Say**:
> "I pick a recipe. It shows what I have and what I need. I can add missing ingredients to my shopping list with one click."

**Key Points**:
- Clear ingredient breakdown
- Shopping list integration
- One-click convenience

---

### Step 6: The "Aha" Moment (30 seconds)

**What to Show**:
- Recipe is ready to cook
- Time saved: "You saved 15 minutes!"
- Option to rate/feedback

**What to Say**:
> "That's it. In under 2 minutes, I went from 'what's for dinner?' to a recipe I can cook right now. The app learns my preferences, so it gets smarter every time I use it."

**Key Points**:
- Time saved (measurable value)
- Learning system (switching cost)
- Complete solution

---

### Step 7: Show Additional Features (1 minute)

**What to Show** (if time):
- Meal planning (if available)
- Family sharing (if available)
- Recipe history
- Mobile app (if available)

**What to Say**:
> "The app also [feature]. And it works on web, iOS, and Android with seamless sync."

**Key Points**:
- Additional value
- Universal platform

## Common Questions During Demo

### "How does it know what I have?"

**Answer**: "You add ingredients via scan, manual entry, or receipt import. The app tracks your pantry and updates as you use items."

### "What if I don't have the ingredients?"

**Answer**: "The app shows what you have vs what you need. You can add missing ingredients to your shopping list with one click."

### "How does it learn my preferences?"

**Answer**: "Every time you rate a recipe or cook it, the app learns. Over time, recommendations get more personalized."

### "What about dietary restrictions?"

**Answer**: "You set your dietary preferences and allergies during onboarding. The AI validates all recipes against your restrictions."

## Demo Recovery Tips

### If Recipe Generation Fails

**Say**: "Let me try that again. Sometimes the AI needs a moment."  
**Do**: Refresh, try again. If still fails, use pre-generated recipe as backup.

### If App is Slow

**Say**: "The app is usually faster - this might be a network issue."  
**Do**: Continue demo, mention it's usually faster.

### If Feature Not Available

**Say**: "That feature is coming soon. The core recipe generation is what we're showing today."  
**Do**: Focus on what works, mention roadmap.
