# Demo Script

**Purpose**: Short phrases founder can say while clicking through demo  
**Format**: What to say + What to click

---

## Opening (30 seconds)

**Say**: "Every day at 6 PM, 50 million Americans stare into their fridge and ask 'What's for dinner?' Current solutions fail because they're recipe-first or planning-first. We solve this by starting with what you actually have."

**Click**: Navigate to landing page

---

## Sign Up (30 seconds)

**Say**: "Signup takes 30 seconds. You can use email or social login."

**Click**: Click "Get Started" → Fill signup form → Submit

---

## Add Pantry (1 minute)

**Say**: "First, we learn what you have. You can scan barcodes, enter manually, or import from receipts."

**Click**: Go to Pantry page → Add 3-5 ingredients (chicken, rice, vegetables)

**Say**: "I'm adding chicken, rice, and some vegetables. This is the pantry-first approach - we start with what you have, not what you need."

---

## Generate Recipe (30 seconds)

**Say**: "Now, the magic. I click 'What's for dinner?'"

**Click**: Click "What's for dinner?" button

**Say**: "In 30 seconds, we get personalized recipe suggestions using what I have. No searching. No planning ahead. Just instant suggestions."

**Wait**: Let recipes generate (should be fast)

---

## Select Recipe (30 seconds)

**Say**: "I pick a recipe - let's say this chicken and rice dish."

**Click**: Click on a recipe

**Say**: "It shows what I have and what I need. I can add missing ingredients to my shopping list with one click."

**Click**: Show ingredient list, highlight "Add to shopping list" button

---

## The "Aha" Moment (30 seconds)

**Say**: "That's it. In under 2 minutes, I went from 'what's for dinner?' to a recipe I can cook right now."

**Click**: Show recipe detail page

**Say**: "The app learns my preferences every time I use it, so it gets smarter. The more you use it, the better it gets."

**Click**: Show rating/feedback option (if available)

---

## Additional Features (1 minute - if time)

**Say**: "The app also supports meal planning, family sharing, and works on web, iOS, and Android with seamless sync."

**Click**: Show additional features (if available)

---

## Closing (30 seconds)

**Say**: "That's What's for Dinner. Pantry-first meal planning with AI that learns. We're solving a daily problem for 50 million Americans."

**Click**: Return to landing page or show key metrics (if available)

---

## Key Phrases (Use Throughout)

- "Pantry-first approach"
- "30-second recipe generation"
- "AI learns preferences"
- "Gets smarter with use"
- "No planning required"
- "Saves 15+ minutes per meal decision"

---

## Timing Guide

- **Opening**: 30 seconds
- **Signup**: 30 seconds
- **Pantry**: 1 minute
- **Generate**: 30 seconds
- **Recipe**: 30 seconds
- **Aha**: 30 seconds
- **Features**: 1 minute (optional)
- **Closing**: 30 seconds

**Total**: ~5 minutes (can shorten to 3 minutes by skipping features)

---

## Practice Tips

1. **Practice timing**: Run through demo 2-3 times
2. **Know your talking points**: Memorize key phrases
3. **Be ready for questions**: See `/demo/DEMO_PATH.md` for common questions
4. **Have backup plan**: Know what to do if something breaks (see `/demo/DEMO_CHECKLIST.md`)
